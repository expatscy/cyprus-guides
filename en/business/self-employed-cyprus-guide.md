You can operate as an individual self-employed professional instead of incorporating a company. Before registering, check your **right to carry out the proposed work**, the activity's professional requirements and the tax and insurance obligations. Registration in one system does not settle all of those questions.

## Who can be self-employed in Cyprus?

Assess the actual residence and work rights that apply to you rather than relying on a general “visa status” table.

- For qualifying family members with a right of residence under EU free-movement rules, [Article 23 of Directive 2004/38](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32004L0038) provides a right to take up employment or self-employment irrespective of nationality. Check whether your situation falls within those rules; the EU-family route and the family route for a Cypriot citizen are distinct.
- The [Digital Nomad scheme](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/en/documents/digital-nomads-and-family-members/) permits remote work for an employer registered abroad **or remote self-employment for clients located abroad**. Do not treat this as permission to serve Cyprus clients. Accompanying family members have no right to employment or economic activity under that family permit.
- For an employer-linked work permit, another family permit or a visitor permit, have the specific permit's conditions checked before starting a business. Do not assume that a spouse permit either universally permits or universally prohibits self-employment.

See the [work and job-search guide](/en/business/working-cyprus-job-search/) and [EU-family residence guide](/en/immigration/eu-citizen-spouse-cyprus-yellow-slip/), and ask the Migration Department about your proposed activity if the conditions are unclear.

## How does registration work?

### Tax registration

The [Tax Department's current individual-registration procedure](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/registration-and-cancellation-from-the-national-tax-registry-issuance-of-a-tax-identification-number-tic-tin/registration-in-the-national-tax-registry-issuance-of-a-tax-identification-number-tic-tin-individuals/) requires initial Tax Registry applications **electronically through Tax For All (TFA)**.

Create a TFA account, then use “Register as a new taxpayer” if you do not have a TIN. If you already have one, use “Apply for access to a tax identification number (TIN)”. Follow the evidence checklist for your category rather than assuming that every applicant submits the same residence document. See the [TIN guide](/en/business/tax-identification-number-cyprus/).

### Social Insurance registration

The [current self-employed insurance service](https://www.gov.cy/service/asfalisi-aftotelos-ergazomenon/) publishes **YKA 1-013**, the self-employed registration application. It also provides district email addresses and instructions for electronically signed applications with the required supporting documents.

Ask Social Insurance to confirm your occupational category, the evidence needed and the appropriate submission route. A generic “same day” or “two weeks for everything” estimate is not a commitment for your application.

### GESY enrolment

[HIO's registration guidance](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hiobeneficiariesregistrationfaq) describes a separate beneficiary enrolment request and category-specific evidence. Where its conditions allow, a personal doctor can assist with enrolment. **Paying contributions and registering with Social Insurance do not automatically establish beneficiary eligibility or complete GESY enrolment.**

## What are the social insurance contributions?

The [official 2026 self-employed contribution table](https://www.mlsi.gov.cy/mlsi/sid/sidv2.nsf/All/CC73852B37301B20C2258E37002C21E4?OpenDocument) specifies **16.6%** for Social Insurance. Its note also states that a **0.5% Human Resource Development Authority levy** on weekly income applies from **5 January 2026**.

The [occupational-category table for 5 January 2026–3 January 2027](https://www.mlsi.gov.cy/mlsi/sid/sidv2.nsf/All/E3FAA841210065C3C2258E37002C0420?OpenDocument) sets different minimum weekly insurable earnings by category and, for some categories, experience. It lists a **€1,325 maximum weekly insurable amount**. Do not substitute the monthly employee limit for the self-employed weekly calculation.

The official service provides **YKA 1-017**, an application to pay contributions based on actual income. Low receipts alone should not be treated as automatic approval to ignore the occupational base: ask Social Insurance about the evidence and decision for your application.

[HIO's financing page](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hiofinancing) lists **4% GESY contributions on self-employed remuneration** and a **€180,000 total annual contribution base per natural person** across the applicable categories. Check the contribution bases, collection rules and your circumstances separately. Adding the percentages does not establish one flat effective rate on all your actual income.

For payments, use the [official Social Insurance payment service](https://www.gov.cy/service/pliromes-eisforon-ypiresion-koinonikon-asfaliseon/) linked from the self-employed service, and confirm the payment period and any other levy due.

## How does income tax work?

For **tax year 2026**, the [Income Tax Law's ordinary individual bands](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_118/full.html) are:

| Portion of annual taxable income | Marginal rate |
|---|---|
| Up to €22,000 | 0% |
| Above €22,000 up to €32,000 | 20% |
| Above €32,000 up to €42,000 | 25% |
| Above €42,000 up to €72,000 | 30% |
| Above €72,000 | 35% |

These are bands of **taxable income**, not an exemption for €22,000 of gross business receipts. Establish the income, allowable deductions and any personal exemptions for the relevant tax year before calculating the liability.

Section 9 permits expenses incurred **wholly and exclusively in producing income**, subject to the law's conditions and exclusions. Do not assume that all rent, internet or equipment purchases are immediately deductible merely because you operate a business. Ask your accountant to classify the actual expenditure.

### Records, accounts and returns

[Section 30 of the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law](https://cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/1978_1_4/full.html) requires invoices and receipts for the relevant business transactions. Its accounts requirements include different provisions for business individuals:

- An exemption from preparation of accounts under section 30(1)(b) where the aggregate annual turnover and the other gross income specified in the provision **does not exceed €120,000**.
- A possible **review**, rather than audit, where that aggregate exceeds €120,000 but does not exceed €200,000 and gross balance-sheet assets do not exceed €500,000, with the specified **two-consecutive-financial-year** condition.
- Audit for cases outside those categories, subject to the provision's further exception powers.

The €500,000 figure is an **asset test**, not a universal turnover threshold. Have an accountant establish which conditions apply; the accounts-preparation exemption is not permission to omit required invoices or other tax compliance.

Keep the tax year and return procedure separate from your registration date. Use the [tax-return guide](/en/business/tax-return-filing-cyprus/) to check the applicable return, payment deadlines and any exemptions rather than assuming that every year uses the same form or deadline. Self-employment income is not administered through an employer's salary PAYE calculation.

## When must I register for VAT and VIES?

The [official business-registration guide](https://www.businessincyprus.gov.cy/doing-business-in-cyprus/start-your-business/registering-for-income-tax-and-value-added-tax/) describes the ordinary Cyprus-established taxable-turnover test: **more than €15,600 during the preceding 12 months**, or an expectation that taxable transactions will exceed €15,600 during the **next 30 days**. This is not simply a calendar-year gross-income test, and other registration rules may apply.

The [Tax Department's VAT-registration FAQ](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/eggrafi-akyrosi-f-p-a/eggrafes-mitrooy-f-p-a/sychnes-erotiseis-gia-tin-eggrafi-f-p-a/) also states that a business not already registered becomes liable to register on the day it provides services to a taxable person in another EU Member State **for which the recipient owes VAT in that Member State**. It requires notification within **30 days** of that obligation arising. This specific rule applies irrespective of the ordinary domestic threshold; it does not mean “every EU client, even before any transaction”.

The current FAQ describes **TD 1101** for VAT registration and prior Tax Registry registration. Check the complete evidence and submission procedure for your case.

The [VIES FAQ](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/paneyropaikes-platformes/vies-vat-information-exchange-system/sychnes-erotiseis-gia-ton-anakefalaiotiko-pinaka-vies/) explains activation through **TFA01** where required, electronic submission through TFA and the reporting conditions. For example, services exempt in the recipient's Member State are not recorded in the summary table. Determine the recipient's status and the actual supply's treatment before deciding what to report.

The [standard Cyprus VAT rate is 19%](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/syntelestes-f-p-a/). Do not call the cross-border recipient-liability rule a universal “0% rate”: check the place of supply, liability and any exemption for the particular service.

## How do I stop or pause activity?

The official self-employed service provides separate forms for:

| Purpose | Form |
|---|---|
| Termination of self-employed activity | YKA 1-015 |
| Declaration of non-employment | YKA 1-016 |
| Request to pay contributions based on actual income | YKA 1-017 |
| Change of occupation | YKA 1-014 |

Ask Social Insurance which procedure applies, what evidence it needs and what periods it accepts. **A period with no revenue is not the same as stopping work**, and a free-form letter should not be assumed to cancel liabilities automatically. Ask separately how the change affects contribution obligations, insurance periods, tax records and any VAT registration.

## Should I work as an individual or form a company?

Compare the actual professional and accounting quotations, liability arrangements, clients, intended employees and retained profits for your activity. A company and an individual do not necessarily have the same income-tax or compliance treatment.

Avoid deciding from a universal setup-cost or “best income level” table. Ask an accountant and lawyer to compare your expected receipts, deductible costs, taxes, contributions, administration and obligations before choosing or changing the structure.

## What should I check before starting?

1. Confirm the proposed activity is permitted under your actual work and residence rights.
2. Obtain the applicable professional licences or approvals and registration instructions.
3. Complete tax and Social Insurance registration and assess GESY eligibility separately.
4. Establish the insurable category, other levies and VAT/VIES obligations for your actual transactions.
5. Keep required records and arrange a timetable for payments, returns and changes of activity.
