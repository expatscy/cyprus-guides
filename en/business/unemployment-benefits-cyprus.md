Cyprus unemployment benefit is administered by **Social Insurance Services**. A contribution record is necessary, but it does not by itself establish eligibility. The current [Social Insurance Law](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html) sets the insurance, unemployment and disqualification conditions.

## Who is eligible?

For the ordinary employee claim, the insurance tests include:

- At least **0.50 actual basic-insurance units**.
- At least **26 weeks elapsed since the week insurance began**.
- At least **0.39 actual or assimilated insurance units in the relevant contribution year**.

These are not a requirement to have worked exactly 26 weeks in the last 12 months. The relevant contribution year is defined by the law's benefit-year rules, rather than automatically being the immediately preceding calendar year. Ask Social Insurance Services to identify the applicable year and check your record. [Third Schedule, paragraph 1 and statutory definitions](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html).

**Self-employed insurance periods are not counted for unemployment benefit**. Ordinary voluntary-insurance periods are also excluded, but insurance for work abroad for a Cypriot employer has separate rules. [Section 24](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html).

For a qualifying unemployment day, you must be **unemployed, capable of and available for work**; approved vocational training has a separate provision. Section 31 sets an age range of **16–65** and excludes a person entitled to a statutory pension, subject to the law's other conditions. It also contains a separate restriction for certain retirees receiving a non-contributory occupational pension, with a subsequent-employment exception. Check these conditions before assuming that age alone establishes eligibility. [Section 31](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html).

Maintain registration with the **Public Employment Service throughout unemployment**. Immigration permission and benefit eligibility are separate assessments: if your right to work or remain depends on your employer, obtain permit-specific advice promptly. [Official unemployment guide](https://www.mlsi.gov.cy/mlsi/sid/sidv2.nsf/All/F1C3ADFCF243FCD7C2257C920046BC15).

## What happens if I resign or refuse a job?

Resignation is not described in the law as an automatic permanent exclusion. Section 34 provides disqualification for **up to six weeks** where a person loses work through their own fault or leaves voluntarily **without reasonable cause**. It also covers refusal or failure, without reasonable cause, to apply for or accept suitable work, take a suitable opportunity, or comply with vocational-training directions. Suitability and reasonable cause require assessment of the facts. [Section 34](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html).

Work stoppages arising from a labour dispute have separate disqualification rules and exceptions. Keep evidence of why the employment ended; do not assume that a particular resignation reason is automatically accepted.

## How much is the benefit?

The weekly amount has **basic and supplementary components**, calculated using the insurance record for the **relevant contribution year**:

- **Basic:** ordinarily **60% of the weekly value of the basic-insurance unit or fraction credited for that year**.
- **Supplementary:** **50% of the weekly value of the supplementary-insurance units**, capped at the **weekly basic insurable-earnings amount**.

Dependant increases attach to the basic component and have their own conditions, including rules concerning a spouse's earnings or benefits and overlapping claims. Request a calculation for your actual record and dependants rather than applying a simple percentage to your latest gross salary. [Fourth Schedule, Part II and section 62](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html).

This guide does not promise a universal monthly maximum or use a salary-based example as an entitlement estimate.

## How long do payments last?

The statutory maximum is **156 benefit days per period of interruption of employment**, not an automatic allowance for every calendar year. **Sundays and other designated days do not count as unemployment days** under section 31. [Sections 31–32](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html).

The ordinary employee waiting period is the **first three days**. For the specified voluntary insurance covering work abroad for a Cypriot employer, the waiting period is **30 days**. All other eligibility conditions still apply.

After exhausting entitlement, the ordinary requalification rule requires **at least 26 weeks of subsequent employment** and contributions on insurable earnings totalling at least **26 times the weekly basic insurable-earnings amount**. Special rules apply to qualifying people aged **60 or over** who are not receiving a pension as defined for that rule. Ask for an assessment before assuming that another six-month claim is available.

## How do I apply?

1. Use the [Public Employment Service's jobseekers platform](https://www.gov.cy/en/service/jobseekers-platform/) to register. Its published process says officers contact you **by telephone to validate the application**. Registration alone is not a benefit award.
2. Apply through the [official unemployment-benefit service](https://www.gov.cy/en/service/unemployment-benefit/). You need an **authenticated CY Login account**; its page describes identity confirmation through online banking, a video call, or a Citizen Service Centre or District Post Office.
3. Follow the application instructions for the evidence needed in your circumstances, and retain submission confirmations.
4. Keep your Public Employment Service registration active throughout unemployment. Confirm any continuing reporting or register-signing requirements with Social Insurance Services: its [FAQ](https://www.mlsi.gov.cy/mlsi/sid/sidv2.nsf/All/4FFC18708B61EC9DC2257A30001FD764) describes signing at intervals determined by the district office.

The official benefit-service contact is **unemployment@sid.mlsi.gov.cy**, with telephone **1450**. Ask about the applicable claim deadline and the effect of any delay promptly; this guide does not establish a seven-day deadline or guarantee backdated payment.

The published guide says benefit payments are made by transfer to the beneficiary's bank account. Confirm the account evidence required by the current application.

## Can I work part-time and receive benefit?

Do not assume that earnings below the benefit amount preserve a partial payment. Section 31 determines whether a day counts as unemployment. It contains specific rules for gainful activity, work normally performed outside usual working hours and a prescribed earnings amount, as well as separate rules for a person's usual weekly work pattern. Report the actual work and pay to Social Insurance Services before claiming for those days. [Section 31(4)](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2010_1_59/full.html).

## What should I do if my employer closes or I lose my job?

Keep the termination notice, payslips, contribution record and correspondence. Ask separately about final wages, leave payments, any contractual thirteenth salary and any redundancy-fund claim.

Assess your residence route separately from the benefit claim. Obtain current Migration Department instructions on employer changes, other statuses and your family's position; do not rely on an informal one-to-three-month grace period or assume that every filing receipt authorises continued residence.

Start your [job search](/en/business/working-cyprus-job-search/) and keep records of applications and official instructions. The [Social Insurance guide](/en/business/social-insurance-cyprus-pension/) explains how to obtain a contribution statement.
