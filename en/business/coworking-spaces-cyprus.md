> **Short answer:** Cyprus has 40+ coworking spaces across Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos, and Larnaca. Prices range from EUR 100-350/month for a dedicated desk, with Limassol being the most expensive.


Whether you are working remotely on a [digital nomad visa](/en/immigration/digital-nomad-visa-cyprus/), running a [Cyprus-registered company](/en/business/company-registration-cyprus/), or simply need a professional space away from home, the island now has over 35 coworking options across every major city. Here is the complete, up-to-date guide.

> **Top 3 picks:**
> - [Hugge Coworking](https://hugge.space/) -- best in Paphos, 24/7 access
> - [SOHO Office Space](https://www.sohocyprus.cy/) -- the go-to in Limassol, 24/7 access
> - [Regus Cyprus](https://www.regus.com/en-cy) -- international network, multiple locations

## What are the alternatives to coworking?

### Virtual office

If you are [registering a Cyprus company](/en/business/company-registration-cyprus/), you need a legal address. A virtual office provides this without renting physical space:
- **Antwork** -- [antwork.com](https://www.antwork.com/) (from around EUR 50/month)
- Company formation agents often include a registered address in their package

### Home office

If your internet connection is reliable, working from home is the cheapest option. Many expats take a hybrid approach -- two or three days at home, two or three in a coworking space.

### Cafes

For occasional work sessions:
- **McDonald's** -- consistent free Wi-Fi across Cyprus (not glamorous, but reliable)
- Independent coffee shops -- most in Limassol, Paphos, and Larnaca town centres have decent Wi-Fi, though power sockets can be scarce

## How much does it cost?

| Type | Typical price |
|------|---------------|
| Hot desk (day) | EUR 15-30 |
| Hot desk (week) | EUR 65+ |
| Hot desk (month) | EUR 150-300 |
| Dedicated desk (month) | EUR 250-400 |
| Private office (month) | EUR 500+ |

Prices vary by city: Limassol is at the higher end, Paphos and Larnaca tend to be cheaper. International chains (Regus, Spaces) are typically more expensive than local operators but include global access.

For comparison, a hot desk in central London runs GBP 200-400 per month. In Cyprus, you are looking at roughly half that -- and often with 24/7 access included.

## How do I choose?

1. **Location** -- pick something close to home. A 45-minute commute to a coworking space defeats the purpose.
2. **Hours** -- do you need 24/7 access, or are standard business hours sufficient? If you work across time zones (common for remote workers serving UK or US clients), evening and weekend access matters.
3. **Internet speed** -- test it on a trial day. Most spaces offer decent speeds, but not all are equal.
4. **Meeting rooms** -- check availability and booking costs if you take client calls or run video meetings regularly.
5. **Community** -- some spaces attract a particular crowd (tech, freelancers, corporate). Visit first to see if it suits you.
6. **Trial day** -- almost every coworking in Cyprus offers a free trial day. Always take it before committing to a monthly plan.
