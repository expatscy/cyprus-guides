> **Short answer:** UK travel advice says crime against tourists is not common, but personal attacks, including sexual assault, happen. Take ordinary precautions; country statistics do not guarantee personal safety.

Use dated statistics to understand their particular measure, and current official warnings to plan your activities. A peace ranking, reported-offence count and road-fatality rate answer different questions.

## What do the crime statistics show?

The Cyprus Statistical Service's [Criminal Statistics 2024](https://www.gov.cy/en/oikonomia/criminal-statistics-2024/), published on 18 December 2025, reports:

| Measure | 2024 figure |
|---|---|
| Serious offences reported to Police | 5,942 |
| Serious cases classified as true | 5,900 |
| Serious crime index: true serious cases per 100,000 inhabitants | 605 |
| Property offences as a share of serious offences | 31.8% |
| Offences against persons as a share of serious offences | 26.8% |

The index was 588 in 2023 and 568 in 2022. These are recorded serious-offence statistics, rather than a measure of every crime or each resident's risk. The methodology records the principal offence when a case contains multiple offences.

An important comparability limit: some domestic-violence offences moved from minor to serious offences in 2024. The offences-against-persons series therefore cannot be directly compared with earlier years without accounting for that change.

Cyprus ranks **80th of 163 countries** in the [Global Peace Index 2026](https://www.visionofhumanity.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Global-Peace-Index-2026-Report.pdf). This measures peace using 23 indicators across conflict, safety and security, and militarisation. It is not a ranking of the safest cities or lowest local crime rates.

## What are the safest areas in Cyprus?

The statistics above do not establish a safest-city ranking. Assess the particular neighbourhood, building, walking routes and transport arrangements before choosing a home.

Visit a prospective area at the times you expect to use it. Check lighting, access to your building, pedestrian crossings and how you will get home. Ask specific questions about a property's security rather than relying on a city's reputation.

## What should women and families consider?

[UK travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/cyprus/safety-and-security) describes personal attacks, including sexual assault, as infrequent but says they happen. It does not guarantee that walking alone at night is safe. Plan transport, keep control of your drinks and seek help if you feel threatened.

For children, check the actual school route and supervise swimming. The same travel advice warns that currents and waves can make swimming unsafe; follow warning signs and swim in approved areas. A beach label does not replace supervision or a check of conditions that day.

## What about LGBTQ+ travellers?

The same [UK travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/cyprus/safety-and-security) states that same-sex sexual activity is legal in the Republic of Cyprus and civil partnerships are recognised. These legal facts do not promise the same social experience in every setting.

## What do road-safety figures show?

The European Commission's [Annual Statistical Report 2026](https://road-safety.transport.ec.europa.eu/document/download/ed29ad84-b2f1-429e-97ca-2b713e7dc1b1_en?filename=ERSOnext_AnnualReport_20260323.pdf), published in March 2026, reports the following **2024** rates:

| Road fatalities per million inhabitants | 2024 |
|---|---|
| Cyprus | 42 |
| EU | 44 |

The report cautions that small national fatality counts can produce considerable annual fluctuations. These population rates do not establish that a particular road, journey or driver is safe.

Allow time to learn unfamiliar junctions and routes. Choose safe pedestrian crossings, use appropriate child restraints and avoid distractions while driving. See the [driving-licence guide](/en/life/driving-license-cyprus/) for licence conditions.

## How should I prepare for earthquakes?

UK travel advice notes that earthquakes and tremors occur. [Cyprus Civil Defence's earthquake instructions](https://www.gov.cy/esoterika-themata/systaseis-politikis-amynas-pros-to-koino-gia-ti-lipsi-metron-aftoprostasias-kai-asfaleias-apo-ton-seismo-2/), published on 4 September 2024, advise:

- Secure furniture and place heavy objects on lower shelves.
- During shaking indoors, shelter under sturdy furniture, kneel and hold a leg of the furniture. Stay away from windows and objects that can fall.
- If sturdy furniture is unavailable, kneel in the middle of the room and protect your head and neck.
- Do not attempt to leave the building during shaking or go onto a balcony. Do not use lifts.
- Outdoors, move away from buildings, poles and other falling-object hazards.
- Expect aftershocks and do not enter damaged buildings until the authorities consider them safe.

Ask a qualified professional about a specific building's seismic condition; this article does not certify buildings or predict earthquake frequency.

## How should I manage heat?

The [Ministry of Health's advice of 14 July 2026](https://www.gov.cy/ygeia/odigies-tou-ypourgeiou-ygeias-gia-prostasia-apo-tis-ypsiles-thermokrasies/) recommends following weather warnings, staying in cool or shaded places, avoiding direct sun from **11:00 to 17:00**, and limiting strenuous activity during hot hours.

Drink fluids unless your doctor has given different instructions. Wear light clothing, a hat and sunglasses, and use sunscreen. Never leave a child, older person or pet in a parked car. Heatstroke is a medical emergency: call **112** if it is suspected.

Research gives context, rather than a prediction for an individual. An [ISGlobal study report](https://www.isglobal.org/en/-/62.700-muertes-asociadas-con-el-calor-del-verano-de-2024) estimated 62,775 heat-related deaths across 32 European countries from 1 June to 30 September 2024, with an estimated Cyprus rate of 236 per million. These are model estimates, not a count of registered heatstroke deaths.

## Are there other natural risks?

[UK travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/cyprus/safety-and-security) warns of high wildfire risk from April to October and advises following official instructions during extreme weather.

Tornadoes also occur: the Department of Meteorology's [February 2024 weather report](https://www.gov.cy/en/agriculture-rural-development-and-environment/monthly-weather-report-february-2024/) records a tornado in Germasogeia on **14 February 2024**, alongside damaging rain and flooding. Do not assume severe weather or flooding is harmless. Check current warnings before travelling.

## What should I know before crossing north?

Check the [north-travel guide](/en/life/northern-cyprus-travel/) for entry conditions and authorised crossings. UK travel advice says cars rented in the Republic often lack insurance cover in the north; confirm the correct cover before crossing. Avoid photographing military installations, where photography can lead to arrest.

## What are the emergency numbers?

For an immediate emergency, call **112**. The [Cyprus Fire Service announcement of 8 May 2026](https://www.gov.cy/dikaiosyni-kai-dimosia-taxi/anakoinosi-schetika-me-tis-kliseis-ektaktis-anagkis-pou-apaiteitai-i-antapokrisi-tis-pyrosvestikis-ypiresias/) says calls requiring Fire Service response, including fires and rescues, must go exclusively through **112**. Give your location and follow the operator's instructions; do not assume a fixed response time.
