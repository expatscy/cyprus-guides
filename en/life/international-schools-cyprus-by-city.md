> **Short answer:** Build a shortlist from the Ministry's named school listings, then check the exact stage, language, programme, admission conditions and full quotation. The tables below group selected entries by **district**; a district includes suburbs and surrounding communities, not just the city centre.

This guide uses the [Ministry's school-directory page](https://www.moec.gov.cy/en/schools_info.html) and its linked [private-secondary directory](https://sch.cy/mc/363/en_private_secondary_general.pdf) and [private-primary directory](https://sch.cy/mc/363/en_private_primary.pdf), checked on **3 October 2026**. It is a selected starting list, not a ranking or a claim to cover every school.

The tables report the Ministry's **listed language of instruction for the secondary entry**. Check actual language streams and subjects with the school; a summary directory does not establish every programme's language, availability or admission requirements. Primary and pre-primary sections need separate checks.

## How much should I budget?

Ask each shortlisted provider for a dated fee schedule and written quotation for the **same academic year, campus and stage**. Check registration, deposits, tuition, books, uniforms, examinations, meals, transport, support, activities and any boarding costs. Ask which charges are compulsory and what happens if you withdraw or transfer.

One concrete example is [PASCAL Lefkosia Secondary School's 2026–2027 schedule](https://www.pascal.ac.cy/fs/resource-manager/view/ce8ade0a-bc3c-497d-a132-4db82cea29b4), updated on **15 December 2025**: Year 1 annual tuition is **€9,300**, and its Year 1 corresponds to UK Year 7. The schedule excludes uniforms, books except those provided free by the Ministry, transport and participation in activities such as competitions and conferences. External examination fees other than GL Assessment are separate. Read its complete compulsory and optional charges and terms.

This example is not a price for all PASCAL pupils or a national minimum or maximum. [PASCAL's fee page](https://www.pascal.ac.cy/admissions/fees) links separate schedules by campus and level. [Island's fee page](https://isl.cy/fees) links its Palodia 2026–2027 schedule and says displayed tuition includes a 30% discount until the permanent campus is ready; confirm the applicable offer before budgeting.

## When and how should I apply?

Private-school dates and requirements should be checked with the named provider. Ask which identity, previous-school, language, health and other documents it requires, and whether any translation or certification is necessary. Confirm assessment, interviews, deposits and available places before withdrawing from another school.

For **public primary** schooling, the [Ministry's registration information](https://www.moec.gov.cy/dde/en/infoserv_primary_general_information.html) describes January registrations on dates announced annually and registration in the pupil's educational region. It sets separate conditions for transfers and relocation. See the [education guide](/en/life/schools-education-cyprus/) for details rather than applying a generic private-school calendar to public registration.

For residence-permit documentation, identify the child's actual category and check its current checklist separately. See the [education guide](/en/life/schools-education-cyprus/) and [residence-permit guide](/en/immigration/residence-permit-cyprus-guide/); a school place alone does not establish immigration permission.

## What public and pre-primary options should I check?

The [Ministry's primary-school information](https://www.moec.gov.cy/dde/en/infoserv_primary_general_information.html) states that public primary attendance is free and compulsory for resident children of all nationalities, regardless of immigration status. Ask the school about the appropriate region, grade and support for your child.

For **2026–2027**, [compulsory pre-primary](https://www.moec.gov.cy/dde/en/infoserv_preprimary_general_information.html) starts at **4 years and 4 months reached before 1 September**. Younger children aged at least three before 1 September may apply, subject to remaining places and selection criteria. The Ministry says [public pre-primary education is free](https://www.moec.gov.cy/dde/en/preprimary_fees.html), while community pre-primary schools set their own fees.

## What about universities?

Use the [Department of Higher Education's institutions page](https://highereducation.ac.cy/en/dae/ekpaideftiko-systima) to identify institutions, then check the particular programme's admission, language, accreditation and fee conditions. Do not extend a school-fee example to university study or assume nationality alone makes every public-university programme free.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much do international schools cost in Cyprus?

Request the fee schedule for the particular school, campus, stage and year. As one dated example, PASCAL Lefkosia Secondary School lists 2026–2027 Year 1 tuition of €9,300, with additional charges and exclusions. This is not a national fee range.

### What schools are available for expat children in Limassol?

The Ministry's Limassol district secondary directory includes Island, SP Triada (Trinity), Foley's, Heritage, Grammar School, PASCAL, Morfosis and Pupils of Pythagoras. These are directory listings, not confirmation of an available place or the programme appropriate for your child.

### When should I apply for schools in Cyprus?

Ask each private school for its current intake dates, assessment process and available places. Public primary registrations normally take place in January on annually announced dates; relocation and transfers have separate conditions. Do not apply one private-school deadline to every provider.
