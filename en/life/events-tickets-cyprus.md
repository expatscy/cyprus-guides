> **Short answer:** Start with the venue or organiser's programme, then follow its ticket link. Check the exact performer, date, ticket category and entry conditions before paying.

## Where should I look for events and tickets?

| Source | What the source provides |
| --- | --- |
| [SoldOut Tickets](https://www.soldoutticketbox.com/en/home) | Event listings and ticket purchases, with music, theatre, dance and children's-event categories |
| [Eventbrite Cyprus](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/cyprus/) | Cyprus event listings, including business and networking events |
| [Luma](https://luma.com/) | Event discovery and event-page creation |
| [Ticket Tailor](https://www.tickettailor.com/) | Online ticketing tools used by event organisers |
| [Visit Cyprus](https://www.visitcyprus.com/) | The Deputy Ministry of Tourism's events listings and calendar |

SoldOut has Greek, English and Russian navigation. Its current English homepage is **soldoutticketbox.com/en/home**. Use the venue's or organiser's link to confirm the seller for a particular event instead of relying on a similar-looking domain.

Event platforms have different listings. Check the programme itself rather than assume one platform covers every Cyprus event or is the island's “main” seller.

## Which venue programmes can I check?

- [Pattihio Theatre](https://www.pattihio.com.cy/) publishes a performance schedule with event dates, ticket prices and ticket links. Its current programme links individual shows to SoldOut or Ticketmaster Cyprus.
- [Rialto Theatre](https://www.rialto.com.cy/) publishes a monthly programme. Its October 2026 listings include the International Short Film Festival of Cyprus at Rialto in Limassol, 10–16 October.

For a club, beach venue or community event, use the organiser's current announcement to confirm the location, schedule and registration process. Do the same for football tickets: check the relevant club's instructions for that match, including any membership or identification requirements. Do not assume a season ticket guarantees priority for every international match.

## How much should I budget for tickets?

Use the listed price for the exact performance and ticket category. Two **Pattihio examples checked on 3 October 2026** illustrate why a universal price range is unhelpful:

- **Bagjan Oktyabr**, 3 October 2026: tickets listed **from €35**.
- **Οικογένεια Κουάξ**, 4 October 2026: tickets listed at **€12**.

These are examples from the [venue's programme](https://www.pattihio.com.cy/), not current availability guarantees or typical prices for all artists. Check the checkout total and any booking charges, concessions or age conditions before purchasing.

## What should I check before booking?

1. **Performer and programme:** read the full event description. A show featuring a composer's music does not by itself mean the composer appears in person.
2. **Date and venue:** confirm the year, start time, exact address and entry instructions.
3. **Ticket category:** check whether it includes a seat, standing entry or a separate add-on.
4. **Conditions:** read age limits, accessibility arrangements, cancellation and refund terms.
5. **Confirmation:** keep the booking receipt and ticket, and check any instructions about identification or transferring a ticket.

If tickets are sold out, ask the organiser about an official waiting list, additional dates or authorised resale. Confirm whether a transferred ticket will be accepted before paying a private seller. For a themed event, use that event's own dress-code instructions.
