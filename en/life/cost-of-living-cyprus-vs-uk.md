> **Short answer:** Cyprus is 25-40% cheaper than the UK overall. Rent is 30-50% lower outside Limassol, groceries are 10-15% cheaper, and dining out costs roughly half. But cars and petrol cost more than in the UK.


Cyprus has long been one of the top destinations for British expats — roughly 60,000 UK nationals live here, concentrated in Paphos and Limassol. But how do the actual costs compare? Here is a side-by-side breakdown of what you will spend in Cyprus versus what you are used to paying in the UK. For standalone Cyprus prices, see our [complete cost of living guide](/en/life/cost-of-living-cyprus-complete-guide/) or the [monthly budget breakdown with April 2026 prices](/en/life/cost-of-living-cyprus-2026/).

> **Sources:** [Numbeo](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/compare_cities.jsp?country1=United+Kingdom&city1=London&country2=Cyprus&city2=Limassol), [Eurostat — Comparative Price Levels](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/prc_ppp_ind/default/table?lang=en), [ONS — Consumer Price Inflation](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices), [CyStat — CPI](https://www.cystat.gov.cy/en/SubthemeStatistics?id=41). Exchange rate: £1 = ~€1.17 (early 2026).

> **Not from the UK?** This guide focuses on UK-to-Cyprus comparisons. If you are relocating from the US, Australia, Canada, or elsewhere, see our [guide for non-UK expats](/en/life/cyprus-for-non-uk-expats/) for specifics relevant to your home country.

## How much is rent?

Rent is the single largest cost difference — and it usually works in Cyprus's favour.

| Property | London (centre) | UK average | Limassol (centre) | Paphos |
|----------|----------------|------------|-------------------|--------|
| 1-bed flat | £1,500–2,200 | £700–1,100 | €1,100–1,650 | €800–1,100 |
| 2-bed flat | £2,200–3,500 | £900–1,400 | €1,500–2,500 | €1,000–1,450 |
| 3-bed house | £3,000–5,000 | £1,100–1,800 | €2,000–3,000 | €1,350–1,800 |

*Prices: Numbeo, Rightmove, early 2026.*

**Key takeaway:** Limassol centre costs roughly 40–50% less than London centre. Paphos is 50–65% cheaper than London and often cheaper than UK regional cities. A couple spending £2,000/month on rent in London can get equivalent or better accommodation in Paphos for €800–1,100.

**Deposit:** UK typically 5 weeks' rent (Tenant Fees Act cap). Cyprus: 1–2 months. More on renting in Cyprus — [rental guide](/en/property/renting-property-cyprus/).

## How do council tax and municipal fees compare?

This is one of the biggest savings for UK expats moving to Cyprus.

| | UK | Cyprus |
|---|---|----|
| Annual cost | £1,200–3,500 (council tax, varies by band/area) | €85–500 (municipal fees + sewage) |
| Based on | Property value band | Flat fee by municipality |
| Includes | Local services, refuse collection | Refuse collection, local services |

*Sources: GOV.UK council tax bands, Cyprus municipal websites.*

**Savings:** £1,000–3,000 per year — a consistent win for Cyprus.

## How does GESY compare to the NHS?

| | NHS (UK) | GESY (Cyprus) |
|---|---------|--------------|
| Cost | Free at point of use (funded by NI contributions) | 2.65% of income (employees); 4% (self-employed) |
| GP visit | Free | €1 co-pay |
| Specialist | Free (but 18-week target often missed) | €6 co-pay |
| Prescriptions | £9.90/item in England (free in Scotland/Wales) | €1 co-pay |
| Dental (NHS) | £23.80–£282.80 per course | Partially covered by GESY |
| Waiting times | Weeks to months for specialists | Generally 1–7 days |

*Sources: NHS.UK, GESY.org.cy, early 2026.*

**Key takeaway:** GESY costs more explicitly (the 2.65% contribution) but delivers faster access. There are no 18-week referral waits. Most UK expats find the trade-off favourable. Many add private insurance (€500–1,500/year) for specialist consultations. Details: [healthcare guide](/en/life/healthcare-cyprus-gesy/).

**UK State Pension note:** Your UK State Pension (£221.20/week full rate, April 2025) is payable in Cyprus and uprated annually because Cyprus is in the EU. This is not the case in some other popular retirement destinations (e.g., Australia, Canada). At current exchange rates, the full State Pension provides roughly €1,070/month — enough to cover basic living costs in Paphos or Larnaca.

| Tomatoes (1 kg) | 2.50–3.00 | 2.00–2.80 | 2.34–3.28 |
| Potatoes (1 kg) | 1.30–1.50 | 1.00–1.40 | 1.17–1.64 |
| Wine (bottle, mid-range) | 6.00–8.00 | 6.00–8.00 | 7.02–9.36 |

*Conversion at £1 = €1.17. Prices: Numbeo, early 2026.*

**Monthly grocery spend:**

| | Cyprus | UK |
|---|--------|-----|
| Single person (home cooking) | €250–400 | £200–350 (~€234–410) |
| Couple | €400–600 | £300–500 (~€351–585) |
| Eating out (meal for two, mid-range) | €50–70 | £50–80 (~€59–94) |

**Key takeaway:** Grocery costs are broadly similar. Cyprus has cheaper local fruit, vegetables, and wine. UK has cheaper dairy and supermarket own-brand products (Tesco, Aldi). The biggest difference: eating out in Cyprus is slightly cheaper, and the quality of local produce (tomatoes, citrus, olive oil) is noticeably higher.

**Tip:** Shop at farmers' markets (laiki) on Wednesdays and Saturdays — 20–30% cheaper than supermarkets for fruit and vegetables.

## How does transport work in Cyprus?

This is where Cyprus is more expensive — and fundamentally different.

| | Cyprus | UK (London) | UK (outside London) |
|---|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| Monthly public transport | €50 (bus only) | £160+ (Zones 1–3 Travelcard) | £50–100 |
| Petrol (1 litre) | €1.35–1.45 | £1.40–1.55 (~€1.64–1.81) | £1.40–1.55 |
| Car insurance (year) | €200–1,200 | £400–1,500 | £400–1,500 |
| MOT (year) | €30–40 | £54.85 | £54.85 |
| Road tax (year) | €50–300 | £0–590 (VED) | £0–590 |

*Sources: Numbeo, TfL, GOV.UK, early 2026.*

**Key takeaway:** Petrol is cheaper in Cyprus than the UK. However, you absolutely need a car — there is no Tube, no rail network, and bus routes are limited. If you currently live in London without a car, add €200–350/month for running one in Cyprus. If you already drive in the UK, your transport costs will likely decrease slightly.

## What are the utility costs?

| Utility | Cyprus (85 m² flat) | UK (85 m² flat) |
|---------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Electricity | €80–250/month | £80–140 (~€94–164) |
| Gas (heating) | No mains gas; €16–22/bottle | £40–80 (~€47–94) |
| Water | €20–40 | £15–40 (~€18–47) |
| Internet (fibre) | €30–50 | £25–45 (~€29–53) |
| **Total** | **€150–310** | **~€188–358** |

*Prices: Numbeo, Ofgem, EAC, early 2026.*

**Key takeaway:** Surprisingly close overall. UK gas bills are high in winter; Cyprus electricity bills are high in summer (air conditioning). Cyprus has no mains gas — cooking and heating use bottled gas or electric. Solar water heaters (standard on most buildings) provide free hot water 8–9 months of the year. More detail: [utilities guide](/en/life/utilities-cyprus/).

## How do taxes compare?

| Tax | UK | Cyprus |
|-----|-----|--------|
| Income tax (top rate) | 45% (over £125,140) | 35% (over €72,000) |
| Tax-free allowance | £12,570 | €22,000 (from 2026) |
| Capital gains tax | 10–20% | 0% on securities; 20% on Cyprus property |
| Dividend tax | 8.75–39.35% | SDC 5%; non-dom exempt for 17 years |
| VAT | 20% | 19% |
| Social Insurance (employee) | 8–13.25% (NI) | 8.8% (SI) + 2.65% (GESY) |

*Sources: GOV.UK, Cyprus Tax Department, early 2026.*

**For retirees:** Foreign pension income is taxed at a flat 5% (for amounts above €3,420/year) under Cyprus's special pension regime — significantly lower than UK income tax rates. Combined with no council tax and lower rent, many UK retirees find their purchasing power increases substantially. See [retirement guide](/en/life/retiring-in-cyprus-guide/).

## What should I factor in before moving?

1. **Currency** — If your income is in GBP (pension, remote work), you benefit from the strong pound. Monitor GBP/EUR rates.
2. **Post-Brexit residency** — UK nationals need a [residence permit](/en/immigration/residence-permit-cyprus-guide/). The process is straightforward but takes a few weeks.
3. **Car is essential** — Budget for buying or importing a car. No rail network, limited buses.
4. **Summer electricity** — Air conditioning can push bills to €200–250/month in July–August. An inverter AC unit saves up to 40%.
5. **Community** — Paphos has the largest British community. English is widely spoken in shops, hospitals, and government offices.
6. **Quality of life** — 340 days of sunshine, low crime, clean air, slower pace. For retirees especially, the lifestyle improvement is the biggest return on your move.

## What are the frequently asked questions?

**Is Cyprus cheaper than the UK?**
Overall, yes — especially compared to London and the South East (30–50% savings). Compared to UK regional towns, costs are roughly similar. The biggest savings: rent, council tax (virtually nil), and eating out. The biggest extra cost: you need a car.

**How much do I need to retire comfortably in Cyprus?**
A couple in Paphos can live comfortably on €2,000–2,800/month. The full UK State Pension (~€1,070/month) covers basic costs. Add private income or savings for a higher standard of living.

**Is the UK State Pension uprated in Cyprus?**
Yes. Because Cyprus is in the EU, your UK State Pension receives annual increases. This is not the case in some other retirement destinations like Australia or Canada.

**What is the biggest hidden cost of moving to Cyprus?**
A car. If you currently live in London without one, add €200–350/month for running a car in Cyprus. Also budget for summer electricity (air conditioning can push bills to €200–250/month in July–August).
