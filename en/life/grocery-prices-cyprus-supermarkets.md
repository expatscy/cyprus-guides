> Grocery shopping in Cyprus: compare the products you actually buy using the government e-Kalathi tool. Use current store prices when planning a monthly budget.

e-Kalathi compares product prices and selected-product baskets by store. Check the basket you need; a universal cheapest-chain ranking is not established by the sources below. [e-Kalathi](https://www.gov.cy/en/service/e-kalathi/).

## Supermarkets and price comparison

Lidl reported its 22nd Cyprus store opening in December 2025. This is a dated network count, not a guarantee of today’s total. Use store locators for the branch you intend to visit.

## Markets and delivery

Larnaca Municipality’s notice of 29 September 2026 lists its Saturday market at 24 Nikolaou Rossou Street, beside the new municipal market, from 04:00 to 14:00.

For Alphamega online orders, the stated minimum is €40 including delivery. The delivery charge is €2.95 below €100; orders above €100 qualify for free delivery. Enter your postcode to check service and select a checkout slot.

## Where can I find kosher food?

The Cyprus Rabbinate lists kosher food venues in several cities, including Larnaca, Limassol, Paphos and Nicosia. Chabad Larnaca lists restaurants and a kosher grocery shop. Check the current certificate and certification level for the venue or product; dairy, fish or vegetables are not automatically certified kosher.

## Budgeting tips

Compare the same product and pack size. Keep dated receipts to estimate your own monthly basket. Check offers, delivery charges and branch hours before shopping. These are planning suggestions, not a claim that a particular chain or market is always cheaper.
