> Compare Cyta, Epic, PrimeTel and Cablenet for home internet and mobile service. Check availability at your exact address before choosing a plan.

As checked on 3 October 2026, Cyta advertises Internet Home Fiber 200 Mbps without telephony at €33/month including VAT and broadband access, plus a €69 connection fee. Fiber availability at your address is required. Check the current offer before ordering. [Cyta](https://www.cyta.com.cy/fiber/en).

## Providers and coverage

Cyta advertises home fiber and mobile services. Its 5G FAQ reports 100% population coverage, which is not a guarantee of signal at every location. A compatible device certified for its network is required.

Epic offers home internet and mobile services. PrimeTel offers home fiber and GIGA Unlimited 5G mobile plans. Cablenet also offers home internet and mobile services.

## eSIM and mobile plans

New Epic prepaid customers can order an eSIM online; existing prepaid customers changing a physical SIM to eSIM are directed to an Epic shop. Epic provides QR-code activation instructions. Cyta offers new prepaid soeasy connections with eSIM online.

Before buying, compare domestic data, the roaming data cap, international-call inclusions, contract term, activation charges and the price after promotions. Calls from Cyprus to foreign numbers are a separate consideration from using your phone while roaming.

## Roaming in the EU

Roam Like at Home generally applies to occasional travel in the EU/EEA using a qualifying subscription from your country of residence or stable links. Calls, texts and data are charged at domestic rates, subject to fair-use conditions. Unlimited or low-cost data plans may have a separate roaming data limit. Permanent use abroad can lead to surcharges.

The rules concern terrestrial mobile networks. Satellite connections on ships or aircraft are excluded; special-rate numbers may cost extra. UK roaming inclusions depend on the operator. Check your plan rather than assume a UK or non-EU SIM includes Cyprus.

## Practical setup

Ask the provider to confirm address coverage, required identity/address documents, installation date and the full contract price. For remote work, test your connection and consider a mobile backup. These are planning suggestions, not performance guarantees.
