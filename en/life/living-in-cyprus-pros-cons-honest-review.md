> **Short answer:** Cyprus may suit your priorities, but compare a specific home, job or business plan, school and healthcare arrangement. Sunshine and particular tax rules are measurable facts; comfort, pace of life and cultural fit are personal judgments.

This is an editorial planning guide, with factual statements tied to the sources below. It is not a survey of residents or a prediction of how quickly you will settle in. Use the advantages and drawbacks as questions to test against your own circumstances.

## What Might Work Well for You?

**Sunshine and seasonal climate.** [Visit Cyprus](https://www.visitcyprus.com/useful-info/climate-weather/) describes long, dry summers from mid-May to mid-October and mild winters from December to February. It reports average bright sunshine of six hours daily in December and January and 11.5 hours over the six summer months. Winter can include rain and snow in the Troodos Mountains. These descriptive averages do not guarantee the weather on a particular visit or a change in your wellbeing.

**A national healthcare system.** GeSY's [contracted-provider services](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hioghshealthcareservices) include personal doctors, outpatient specialists, pharmaceuticals and inpatient care. Access depends on the [beneficiary category](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hiobeneficiaryeligiblility) and [registration in the Beneficiary Record and a Personal Doctor List](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hiobeneficiariesregistrationfaq). Treat confirmed access to the services you need as an advantage; do not assume that moving here automatically enrols you or makes every service free.

**Tax rules that may suit your circumstances.** The standard corporate tax rate is **15% from 2026**, replacing 12.5%. This is a company rate, not a promise about your personal income tax or total business costs. [Official business tax-reform information](https://www.gov.cy/taxreform/forologika-ofeli-gia-epixeiriseis-nomika-proswpa/) sets out the change.

The [Tax Department's domicile guidance](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/tax-residency-domicility/) explains the non-dom SDC exemption on dividends. Tax residence and domicile must be assessed; this is not an automatic 17-year exemption for every newcomer. A person tax-resident for at least **17 of the preceding 20 years** is deemed domiciled in Cyprus irrespective of domicile of origin. GeSY contributions may still apply to relevant income under their own rules.

For qualifying intellectual-property profits, the [official incentives guidance](https://www.gov.cy/mof/documents/forologiki-politiki-kai-metarrythmiseis-2/forologika-kinitra/) describes an **80% deduction**, subject to eligible intangible assets, the nexus calculation and other conditions. Where the entire relevant profit qualifies, applying the 15% standard company rate to the remaining 20% gives a **3% corporate-tax component**. Do not apply that arithmetic to all company income or treat it as the complete tax bill.

**Language may help with settling in.** The [tourism board's language information](https://www.visitcyprus.com/useful-info/languages-religions/) describes English as widely spoken. That does not guarantee an English-speaking staff member, form or service in every setting. Check your school, employer, clinician or authority's language arrangements, and consider [learning Greek](/en/life/learning-greek-language-cyprus/) for your own goals.

## What Should You Test Before Moving?

**Heat and the home you will live in.** The climate guide gives July–August average maximum temperatures of about **38°C on the central plain and 27°C in the Troodos Mountains**. These are averages, not an upper limit or a forecast for every city. The [Ministry of Health's heat advice](https://www.gov.cy/ygeia/odigies-tou-ypourgeiou-ygeias-gia-prostasia-apo-tis-ypsiles-thermokrasies/) recommends avoiding prolonged sun exposure, particularly **11:00–17:00**, and limiting strenuous work or exercise during the hottest hours. Discuss heat-sensitive medical needs with your clinician.

Inspect cooling, shade and the home's energy needs. Build an electricity estimate from your expected consumption and the applicable tariff, rather than assuming every summer bill falls within one fixed range. Our [utilities guide](/en/life/utilities-cyprus/) explains the cost components.

**Safety without a blanket guarantee.** Eurostat's **2024** data records **nine police-recorded intentional homicide offences in Cyprus**, or **0.93 per 100,000 inhabitants**. Those are dated national police-recorded offence statistics, not a measure of every type of violence or a guarantee about a particular street, school journey or person. Avoid treating them as proof that children can travel independently in every neighbourhood. See the [safety guide](/en/life/is-cyprus-safe-crime-safety-guide/) for other risks and source limitations.

**Administrative requirements.** Check the actual application channel, documents, appointments and official examination periods for your residence, tax, vehicle or utility procedure. Plan around the current instructions and keep copies. Do not assume that every service is online, that every service requires repeated visits, or that an anecdotal delay is a standard processing time. Start with the [first 90 days guide](/en/life/first-90-days-cyprus-reality/).

**Healthcare for an ongoing condition.** Before committing, ask which consultant, tests, treatment and follow-up you can access, whether the provider is contracted with GeSY, and what insurance or referral conditions apply. Discuss any possible treatment abroad with your care team and insurer. Confirm the arrangements for your specific condition with the treating team.

**Your daily journey and travel needs.** Test the route from your proposed home to work, school and healthcare at the times you will use it. Check transport schedules, licence recognition and current driving requirements. For visits to family, compare the actual route, seasonal availability, fare and connections; a single flight duration is not a reliable door-to-door estimate.

**Career and cultural fit.** Look at specific vacancies, employers and after-tax offers for your field. Review the events, arts, food and social activities you would use. If access to a particular professional network or cultural programme matters, confirm it before moving rather than relying on a city-wide stereotype. Our [events guide](/en/life/events-tickets-cyprus/) gives practical booking checks.

**Support and wellbeing.** Consider how you will keep contact with family, build local support and continue any care you already use. If wellbeing is part of your reason for moving, discuss your plan with an appropriate professional and trusted people. Do not budget your emotional adjustment around a fixed honeymoon period or expect sunshine alone to resolve a health condition.

## Who Might Find the Trade-Off Worthwhile?

- **Remote workers:** Confirm the residence route, permitted work, tax position and internet service at the actual address. Decide whether the working environment suits you.
- **Retirees:** Check residence eligibility, healthcare access, housing and travel to your support network. Our [retirement guide](/en/life/retiring-in-cyprus-guide/) explains the routes and conditions.
- **Families:** Confirm the school place, childcare arrangements, healthcare registration and home-to-school journey. Use the [family guide](/en/life/moving-to-cyprus-with-children/) before committing.
- **Company founders:** Compare the full company and personal tax position, compliance costs and practical business needs. A headline rate alone is not enough to decide.

Weigh these planning considerations according to your own priorities.

## Should You Move?

Make a short list of what you need, what you would like and what would make the move unworkable. Test the actual home, services and routine, including conditions you find difficult. A visit during a hot period may help you judge heat tolerance; it cannot guarantee that you will enjoy the rest of the year.

Use written housing, school, insurance and employment offers to build your budget. Keep the personal preference decision separate from the facts you can confirm. See our [cost guide](/en/life/cost-of-living-cyprus-2026/) for budget planning.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the main advantages of living in Cyprus?

Potential attractions include sunshine, access to GeSY when eligible and registered, and tax rules that may suit particular circumstances. Visit Cyprus describes average bright sunshine of six hours daily in December–January and 11.5 hours over the six summer months. The standard corporate tax rate is 15% from 2026; non-dom dividend SDC relief depends on tax residence and domicile.

### What are the biggest downsides of living in Cyprus?

The main questions to test are heat tolerance, the cost and suitability of housing, your daily travel, administrative requirements, career options and access to the healthcare you need. These depend on your circumstances. The Ministry of Health advises avoiding prolonged sun exposure particularly between 11:00 and 17:00 during heat, rather than treating every outdoor activity as impossible all summer.

### Who should reconsider moving to Cyprus?

Reconsider if your required job, healthcare, school place or budget has not been confirmed, or if the summer climate conflicts with your needs. Check actual providers and offers. Base the decision on confirmed details and your own needs.

### Is Cyprus a good place to retire?

It depends on your residence route, healthcare eligibility and registration, housing budget, heat tolerance, support network and travel needs. Confirm those before committing. A tax or GeSY contribution rate does not itself establish healthcare access; ask the relevant authority and provider about your circumstances.
