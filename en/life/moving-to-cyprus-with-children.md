> **Short answer:** Plan school admission, healthcare registration and residence documents separately. Public primary education is free; private-school fees and residence requirements must be checked for the specific school and permit route.

Moving with children means coordinating several decisions. Start with your child's year group and school place, then check healthcare access and the residence route before committing to housing.

## How Do I Choose the Right School for My Child?

**Public schools:** Public primary education is free and compulsory for resident children of all nationalities, regardless of legal or illegal immigration status. First-grade entry is at age six before 1 September. Registration normally takes place in January on dates announced each year. Relocation transfers follow a separate procedure through the receiving school; other transfer requests involve the District Education Office. Do not plan around unrestricted admission at any time.

For first primary registration, the Ministry lists a birth certificate for children born in Cyprus; for children born abroad, an Alien Registration Certificate or an official document from the country of birth. Ask the school which additional records it needs for your child's placement.

**Pre-primary education:** For 2026/27, compulsory entry is at four years and four months before 1 September. Younger children aged at least three before 1 September may be admitted if places remain after compulsory-age registrations and transfers, subject to selection criteria. Public pre-primary attendance is free; community kindergartens set their own fees. These are pre-primary-school rules, not a universal nursery entitlement for babies and toddlers.

**Private schools:** The Ministry lists private primary and secondary schools separately, with their languages and locations. [Foley's](https://foleysschool.com/foleys-school/) describes its curriculum as broadly based on the UK National Curriculum, with IGCSEs and A Levels. [Heritage](https://www.heritageschool.ac.cy/secondary-curriculum) describes GCSE/IGCSE and AS/A Level stages. Check exam boards, subject combinations and the receiving university's entry requirements rather than assuming automatic acceptance of every qualification.

Use our [school comparison](/en/life/schools-cyprus-comparison/) and [city directory](/en/life/international-schools-cyprus-by-city/) to shortlist schools. Request the current fee schedule for the exact campus and year group, including registration, books, uniform, transport and examination charges. Ask about language support and placement rather than expecting a fixed adaptation period.

## How Does Children's Healthcare Work in Cyprus?

[HIO's beneficiary categories](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hiobeneficiaryeligiblility) include eligible dependants who are ordinarily resident in Republic-controlled areas. For family members of a non-EU beneficiary who qualifies through permanent residence, those family members must also hold permanent residence. Nationality, ordinary residence and the applicable beneficiary category matter; a parent's work permit alone is not an automatic guarantee.

To access GeSY services, the child must be enrolled in the Beneficiary Record and a Personal Doctor List. HIO distinguishes this registration from the automatic linking of a registered minor's medical record to a parent's portal account, which itself has conditions. Follow the [registration instructions](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hiobeneficiariesregistrationfaq) and ask HIO to resolve your family's category.

GeSY's services through contracted providers include personal doctors, outpatient specialists, pharmaceuticals, inpatient care and preventive dentistry. Access and charges depend on the service rules; coverage does not mean every treatment is free. The [published dental catalogue](https://www.gesy.org.cy/el-gr/announcementdef/20201218-dentists-activity-catalogue.pdf) provides age-specific preventive services, including for beneficiaries aged 12 and over, rather than an under-16-only benefit.

If using private insurance, obtain a quote for your family's ages and needs, and compare exclusions, waiting periods and covered providers. Match it to the residence checklist: the digital-nomad dependent-child checklist, for example, requires inpatient and outpatient cover and transportation of a corpse. Confirm how the insurer meets that requirement rather than assuming a universal family premium.

Bring vaccination and medical records and ask the child's clinician to review them and advise on any catch-up care. Confirm appointment availability and charges directly; do not assume public emergency care is free for every insurance or residence status.

See our [healthcare guide](/en/life/healthcare-cyprus-gesy/) and [GeSY comparison](/en/life/gesy-vs-private-healthcare-cyprus/) for service and eligibility details.

## What Documents Do Children Need for a Residence Permit?

Select your route before preparing documents. The [digital-nomad scheme](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/en/documents/digital-nomads-and-family-members/) includes underage children and requires family members to obtain temporary residence permits. Its dependent-child checklist is an example, not a checklist for every family:

- Passport validity of at least three months from application.
- The sponsor's child's birth certificate.
- A custody decision or the other parent's consent where applicable.
- Health insurance for inpatient and outpatient care and transportation of a corpse.
- School registration or attendance evidence from an institution in Cyprus or abroad only where the child is a pupil or student.

Check the full official checklist for the remaining financial, accommodation, medical and other requirements, and distinguish an initial application from renewal. The checklist's legacy reference to stamping rental agreements should be checked against current law; do not treat its wording as confirmation of a current stamp-duty charge.

Document certification also depends on the document and issuing country. Under Regulation (EU) 2016/1191, qualifying public birth documents issued in one EU Member State and presented to another Member State's public authorities are exempt from apostille. For certain documents, an attached multilingual standard form can avoid translation requirements; a certified translation made in an EU Member State must be accepted. For other cases, check the applicable certification and translation procedure.

Ask the school about certificate arrangements before a holiday closure and coordinate them with your application date. See the [residence permit guide](/en/immigration/residence-permit-cyprus-guide/) and [apostille guide](/en/documents/apostille-documents-cyprus/) for the relevant procedures.

## Which Areas Should Families Compare?

The Ministry's private-school directories list schools in Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos and Larnaca districts. A district listing does not establish that a school is in the city centre or has a place in your child's year group.

Shortlist a school and test the actual journey from prospective homes. Compare housing quotes, transport, childcare hours and access to your chosen healthcare provider. Visit an area at the times your family will use it; city-wide rankings and a fixed rent difference are a poor substitute for checking the specific home and campus.

For neighbourhood planning, see [Limassol](/en/life/best-neighborhoods-limassol/), [Paphos](/en/life/paphos-neighbourhoods-guide-2026/) and [Larnaca](/en/life/larnaca-neighbourhoods-guide-2026/).

## What Activities and Childcare Should I Arrange?

Ask each childcare provider for its accepted ages, registration status, current fees, daily hours and holiday closures. Check meals, collection arrangements and how staff handle your child's medical or language needs. Do not assume pre-primary rules also cover a nursery or private childcare arrangement.

For after-school activities, ask the school or local provider about age groups, teaching language, safeguarding, transport and term dates. Get written prices for sports, arts, language tuition or camps before budgeting. Check the calendar for your child's school and year group rather than using one summer-holiday duration for every school.

## What About Child Benefits and Financial Support?

The [child benefit and single-parent benefit service](https://www.gov.cy/en/service/child-benefit-and-single-parent-family-benefit/) is administered by the **Welfare Benefits Administration Service**, not Social Insurance Services. Its online application requires Cy Login registration and an authenticated profile.

The [current Child Benefit Law](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_167/full.html), sections 2–7, sets dependent-child and family definitions, residence, income and asset criteria, and application requirements. Its basic residence rule is at least five years of legal and continuous residence in Republic-controlled areas before application. A newly arrived legal resident should not assume entitlement. Single-parent benefit is subject to its own definition and linked eligibility conditions.

Check your family's circumstances with the service before counting a benefit in your relocation budget. Do not assume a universal monthly amount or payment frequency; the law distinguishes families by the number of dependent children and sets the applicable payment rules.

## What Tips Help Families Relocating?

1. Arrange a school visit and ask about placement before signing a long-term lease.
2. Prepare the child's records and check the required certification before paying for translations or apostilles.
3. Confirm GeSY eligibility and registration, or the insurance your route requires.
4. Build a budget from written school, housing, childcare and insurance quotes.
5. Make a plan for school closures, transport and emergency contacts.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I enrol my child in a school in Cyprus?

Public primary education is free and compulsory for resident children of all nationalities, regardless of immigration status. Registration normally takes place in January on annually announced dates; relocation transfers have a separate procedure. For private schools, check the provider’s admissions requirements and the fee schedule for the exact campus, year group and academic year.

### Are children covered by GeSY healthcare in Cyprus?

A child must meet a GeSY beneficiary category and be registered in the Beneficiary Record and a Personal Doctor List. Eligible dependants ordinarily resident in Republic-controlled areas can qualify, but dependants of a non-EU permanent-resident beneficiary must also have permanent residence. Coverage is not automatic merely because a parent has a work permit.

### What documents do I need for my child’s residence permit in Cyprus?

Use the current checklist for your family’s residence route. For digital-nomad dependent children, the published checklist requests a passport valid at least three months from application, a birth certificate, conditional custody or parental-consent evidence, and school registration or attendance evidence only for pupils or students. Other routes have different requirements.

### How should I choose an area for my family?

Compare the specific school campus, your daily journey, housing quotes and access to healthcare. The Ministry’s private-school directories list options in Limassol, Nicosia, Paphos and Larnaca districts. Check the language, year group, available place and current fees directly with each school.
