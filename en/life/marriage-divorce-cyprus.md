> **Short answer:** Getting married in Cyprus requires a Notice of Marriage, with the ceremony no earlier than 15 days later; the Limassol Municipality charges EUR 128 for the notice (checked 2026-11-07). Bring birth certificates, passport, and proof of marital status. Church weddings require additional Orthodox documentation.


Marriage and family documents are important for many immigration procedures in Cyprus. Whether you are planning to get married on the island or need to validate an existing marriage for residence purposes, this guide covers documents, costs, timelines, and legal processes.

## How do I get married in Cyprus?

### Civil marriage

Cyprus is one of the most popular destinations for civil marriages in Europe. The process is straightforward, available to all nationalities and religions, and does not require residency. Many British expats and international couples choose Cyprus for its simplicity compared to UK or home-country bureaucracy.

### Where does the ceremony take place?

- **Municipality (Town Hall)** — civil ceremony. Available in most cities: Nicosia, Limassol, Paphos, Larnaca, Ayia Napa.
- **Special venues** — some municipalities allow ceremonies outside the building (hotels, beaches) with prior arrangement.
- **Church** — religious ceremony (only for certain Christian denominations).

### Required documents

- **Passports** of both parties (valid)
- **Birth certificates** with [apostille](/en/documents/apostille-documents-cyprus/) (from your home country)
- **Certificate of no impediment** (proof you are not currently married) — from your country's registry or embassy, with apostille
- **Divorce certificate** — if previously married (with apostille)
- **Two witnesses** — over 18 (some municipalities provide witnesses)

### Language

From community experience:

> "Everything in English (only the first marriage certificate in Greek), in 2022, Germasogeia municipality."

Most municipalities conduct ceremonies in English. The marriage certificate is issued in Greek — an English copy is available on request.

## How much does a wedding cost in Cyprus?

| Item | Approximate Cost |
|------|------|
| Notice of Marriage (ceremony no earlier than 15 days later) | EUR 128 |
| Notice of Special Procedure (earlier date, urgent cases) | EUR 282 |
| Certified copy of the marriage certificate | EUR 14 |
| Extra charge for a Saturday or out-of-hours ceremony | EUR 130 |

Fees above are from the Limassol Municipality ([official page](https://www.limassol.org.cy/en/civil-weddings), checked 2026-11-07); other municipalities may differ. Ceremonies at other venues cost extra, and church or reception costs are not set by the municipality.

**Timeline:** Both parties submit the Notice of Marriage in person, then the ceremony takes place no earlier than 15 days later. The Limassol Municipality advises planning to stay in Cyprus for about 20 days.

## Is a Cyprus marriage recognised in my home country?

**Generally yes.** Civil marriages performed in Cyprus are recognised by most countries under the Hague Convention. You will need:

- Cyprus marriage certificate with **apostille**
- **Certified translation** into your home country's language
- Registration at your home country's registry (varies by country)

**For British expats:** A Cyprus civil marriage is fully recognised in the UK. Register it with the General Register Office if you wish.

## How do I validate a foreign marriage in Cyprus?

### For immigration purposes

When applying for a [spouse residence permit](/en/immigration/eu-citizen-spouse-cyprus-yellow-slip/):

- **Marriage certificate** from the country where you married
- **Apostille** (if married outside Cyprus)
- **Translation** to Greek or English (certified)

### For Schengen visas

Consulates may require:

- Apostilled marriage certificate
- Sometimes — a **handwritten declaration** about traveling together

> "They required an apostilled marriage certificate. And a handwritten note about traveling together."

## How does divorce work in Cyprus?

### Divorce through Family Court

In Cyprus, divorce is possible **only through the Family Court**. There is no administrative or out-of-court divorce.

**By mutual consent:**
- Both parties file a joint petition
- Conditions and timing depend on the case; confirm them with the Family Court or a lawyer

**Contested divorce:**
- One party files a petition
- Grounds: adultery, domestic violence, abandonment, prolonged separation
- Duration and legal costs depend on the case; ask a lawyer

**Important:** Cyprus family law applies regardless of the nationalities of the spouses if the divorce takes place in Cyprus.

### If divorced abroad

- **Divorce certificate** with apostille
- Translation to Greek or English
- If documents are missing — an **affidavit** (sworn declaration) is accepted

> "I provided affidavits about the divorces."

### For citizenship

When applying for [citizenship](/en/immigration/cyprus-citizenship-naturalization/) (M127), they may ask about previous marriages — documents or affidavits needed.

## Children in divorce — what matters for immigration?

### Second parent's consent

If parents are divorced and the child lives in Cyprus:

- **Power of attorney** from the second parent — mandatory
- Required for living abroad, school enrolment, traveling with the child

> "In case of separated living, there must be a power of attorney from the second parent for everything: living abroad, schooling, etc."

Original power of attorney stays with you — certified copies go to all authorities.

**For British families:** If the divorce was in the UK, ensure the court order is apostilled and translated. UK court orders are recognised in Cyprus but require authentication.

## How does a name change work after marriage or divorce?

### Upon marriage

If changing your surname:

- All documents (residence permit, insurance, bank accounts) need updating
- For old documents — an affidavit explaining the change

### For citizenship

When applying for citizenship, **parents' surnames at birth** are indicated:

- If mother/father changed surname — affidavit required

## What practical tips should I know?

1. **Apostille in advance** — while still in your home country. UK: via the Legalisation Office. EU: via the relevant authority.
2. **Get certified translations** — only from qualified translators (in Cyprus or your home country)
3. **Keep originals** — copies for submission, originals in a safe place
4. **Prepare affidavits** — if documents are missing, an affidavit is an accepted solution
5. **Plan about 20 days** — the Limassol Municipality advises this for a marriage by notice
6. **Check with the municipality** — each municipality may have slightly different requirements
