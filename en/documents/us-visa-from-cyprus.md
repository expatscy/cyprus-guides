US visa applications based on Cyprus residence require proof of that residence. **Eligible British citizens** with the unrestricted right of permanent abode in the UK and specified islands may use the Visa Waiver Program for visits of up to 90 days, with ESTA approval and all other VWP requirements; a UK passport alone does not establish eligibility. This guide covers B1/B2 applications.

## What do I need to know?

### Where to Apply

**US Embassy in Nicosia:**
- Metochiou & Ploutarchou Street
- Engomi, Nicosia 2407

### Visa Types

- **B1/B2** — tourism and business
- Check the visa category that matches your actual purpose; a visitor visa does not permit ordinary employment or degree study

## How do I book an interview?

### How to Book

1. Fill out form **DS-160** online
2. Follow the consulate’s instructions for fee payment and interview scheduling

### Wait Times

Check current appointment availability:

- Check the [State Department global wait-time table](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/global-visa-wait-times.html); figures are estimates, not appointment guarantees
- Availability changes; check the booking system before making plans

### For Third Country Nationals

Applicants who are not Cypriot citizens may apply on the basis of Cyprus residence:

- Can apply in Nicosia
- Demonstrate **residence in Cyprus**; this is separate from proving visitor-visa eligibility

## What documents do I need?

### Required

- Passport valid at least six months beyond the intended US stay, unless a country-specific agreement provides an exemption
- Upload a photo meeting US requirements with DS-160; bring a printed photo if upload fails
- DS-160 confirmation
- Fee payment receipt, if payment is required before the interview

### Additional Evidence

- Evidence of the purpose of your trip
- Evidence of your intent to depart after the trip, such as employment or family ties
- Evidence of your ability to cover trip costs, including support from another person if relevant

## What happens at the interview?

### What You Need to Establish

- Your permitted visit purpose
- Your intent to depart after the temporary visit
- Your ability to pay the trip costs

### Tips

- Prepare accurate answers about your circumstances
- Gather relevant evidence of your employment or family ties

## What special considerations apply?

### Case-Specific Processing

- Some cases require **additional administrative processing** after the interview
- Its duration depends on the individual case
- Do not use an undated nationality-based estimate to plan travel

### Administrative Processing

Some applications go for **additional review**:

- There is no fixed published duration for an individual case
- Follow the consulate’s instructions about passport retention or return

## What if my application is rejected?

### If Rejected

- Can apply **again**
- A new application normally requires a new fee; 221(g) cases may instead allow the requested documents to complete the existing case
- For 214(b), identify relevant additional information or significant changes before reapplying

### 214(b)

A refusal under **Section 214(b)** may mean:

- The applicant did not establish eligibility for the visa category
- The applicant did not overcome the presumption of immigrant intent

## What practical tips should I know?

1. **Book in advance** — queue can be long
2. **Prepare relevant evidence** — trip purpose, intent to depart and ability to pay are assessed individually
3. **Be honest** — willful misrepresentation of a material fact or fraud may cause permanent visa refusal or denial of entry
4. **Explain your circumstances accurately** — previous travel does not guarantee approval

## How long does the US visa process take from Cyprus?

Separate appointment availability from the decision and passport-return stages:

| Stage | Duration |
|-------|----------|
| DS-160 form completion | Depends on the applicant |
| Fee payment and appointment booking | Follow current local instructions |
| Wait for interview slot | Check current official wait-time estimates and availability |
| Interview day | Follow the appointment instructions |
| Decision and passport return | Case-specific; follow consular instructions |
| Administrative processing (if applicable) | Case-specific; no fixed duration |

Apply early. The official estimates do not establish a reliable “best month” for every applicant.

## What does the cost breakdown look like?

| Fee | Amount |
|-----|--------|
| B1/B2 consular fee | **$185** |

The application fee is **non-refundable** even if the visa is denied. Follow the Embassy’s current payment instructions. A nationality-specific issuance fee may also apply.

## What should I bring to the interview?

Additional documents may be requested to establish your visit purpose, intent to depart and ability to pay. Employment and family ties may support the application; follow any case-specific request.

## What happens after the interview?

**If approved:** Follow the consulate’s instructions for any issuance fee and passport/visa return. Do not rely on a fixed collection deadline or assume a particular delivery option.

**If refused under 214(b):** There is no appeal process. A new application requires a new form, fee and interview; provide additional relevant information or evidence of significant changes in circumstances.

**If administrative processing is required:** Follow the consulate’s instructions. Timing depends on the individual case. Check the [official CEAC visa status service](https://ceac.state.gov/CEACStatTracker/Status.aspx?App=NIV) using the application details requested there.
