## What transfer fees apply to resale properties?

The ordinary transfer-fee discount does not eliminate the fee.

Transfer fees are paid at the Land Registry when the title deed is transferred. The progressive scale below assumes the ordinary 50% discount. It does not apply where the seller acquired the property through the specified foreclosure procedures in Parts VI/VIA of Law 9/1965:

| Property Value | Standard Rate | Discounted Rate |
|---------------|--------------|-----------------|
| First EUR 85,000 | 3% | **1.5%** |
| EUR 85,001-170,000 | 5% | **2.5%** |
| Above EUR 170,000 | 8% | **4%** |

**Calculation — EUR 300,000 resale apartment, ordinary discount:**
- EUR 85,000 x 1.5% = EUR 1,275
- EUR 85,000 x 2.5% = EUR 2,125
- EUR 130,000 x 4% = EUR 5,200
- **Total: EUR 8,600** (2.87% of purchase price)

This fee is based on the **declared transaction value**, and the DLS may assess a higher market value if they believe the price is below market. If the DLS assessment is higher, you pay fees on the higher number.

**New builds where VAT was paid:** Transfer fees are exempt entirely. The exemption depends on VAT being charged on the same transaction.

Source: [DLS fee calculator](https://portal.dls.moi.gov.cy/en/alles-ypiresies/ergaleia/ypologismos-telon-metavivasis/)

## Why is the 5% VAT not really 5%?

The reduced rate has specific eligibility and calculation conditions (Law 42(I)/2023, effective June 2023):

### Ordinary eligibility limits

| Condition | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Eligible area at 5% | First **130 m²** only |
| Maximum area | **190 m²** total |
| Area 131-190 m² | Taxed at **19%** |
| Max value at 5% rate | **EUR 350,000** |
| Max total property value | **EUR 475,000** |
| Residence commitment | **10 years** |
| Eligibility | Qualifying primary permanent residence; individual aged 18+; no other reduced-rate dwelling; replacement possible after repayment compliance |

Special rules apply for qualifying disabled beneficiaries and families with at least four children; see the [property-tax guide](/en/property/property-taxes-cyprus-vat-cgt/) and the VAT Act.

### What This Means in Practice

**Scenario: 170 m² apartment at EUR 400,000**

Assume an eligible primary-residence buyer and the ordinary post2023 scheme:

- First 130 m² (approximately EUR 267,647.06 of value): 5% = **EUR 13,382.35**
- Remaining taxable value (approximately EUR 132,352.94 of value): 19% = **EUR 25,147.06**
- **Total VAT: EUR 38,529.41** — an effective rate of **9.6%**, not 5%

**Scenario: 100 m² apartment at EUR 250,000 (within all limits)**

- 100 m² at 5% = **EUR 12,500**
- This is genuinely 5% — but only because the property is under all thresholds

### The 10-Year Trap

If primary-residence use ceases within ten years, notify the Tax Department and repay the proportional VAT saving **within 30 days**. Exceptions apply upon death or transfer to an eligible adult child. The table assumes the entire €300,000 price originally qualified for 5% VAT.

You commit to living in the property for 10 years. If you rent it out, sell it, or move out before that:

1. You must notify the Tax Department within **30 days**
2. You repay the VAT difference (14%) **proportionally to remaining years**

| Years before change | Clawback rate | On EUR 300,000 property |
|--------------------|---------------|------------------------|
| After 1 year | 14% x 9/10 = 12.6% | **EUR 37,800** |
| After 3 years | 14% x 7/10 = 9.8% | **EUR 29,400** |
| After 5 years | 14% x 5/10 = 7.0% | **EUR 21,000** |
| After 7 years | 14% x 3/10 = 4.2% | **EUR 12,600** |
| After 9 years | 14% x 1/10 = 1.4% | **EUR 4,200** |

Review how a change in your residence use would affect the original benefit. The statutory exceptions and the actual amount of the original VAT benefit must be checked.

Source: [Tax For All VAT calculator](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tfa/en/documents/vat-5-calculation-tool-for-the-purchase-building-of-a-house/)

### Transitional Scheme Deadline

If the town-planning permit application was submitted by **31 October 2023**, the old 5% rules may apply (first 200 m², no cap). The **15 June 2026** deadline has passed for projects with building permits issued by **31 December 2024**; under VAT Act section 63, the Commissioner may examine qualifying declarations through **31 December 2026** where planning-authority delays prevented timely completion. Confirm your project's eligibility with the Tax Department.

## What common expenses recur monthly?

Under Cap. 224 section 38ΙΑ, owners of units in jointly owned buildings contribute to necessary insurance, maintenance, repair and management costs. Check the actual budget for items such as:

- Building insurance
- Cleaning of common areas
- Elevator maintenance
- Garden and landscape maintenance
- Swimming pool chemicals and equipment
- Lighting of common areas
- Management company fee

### Hypothetical monthly budget inputs

| Property Type | Monthly Common Expenses |
|--------------|------------------------|
| Standard apartment (no pool) | EUR 50-80 |
| Apartment complex with pool | EUR 80-150 |
| Luxury development (pool, gym, concierge) | EUR 150-400 |
| Premium tower example | EUR 300-600+ |
| Townhouse in gated community | EUR 100-200 |
| Detached villa example, no shared-property contribution assumed | EUR 0; private maintenance excluded |

**Annual impact, assuming an unchanged contribution:** A EUR 120/month common expense = EUR 1,440/year = EUR 14,400 over 10 years. On a EUR 250,000 apartment, that is 5.8% of the purchase price in maintenance alone.

Section 38ΚΗ permits the management committee to set contributions and payment arrangements for the statutory purposes. Ask for the building regulations, budget, meeting decisions and contribution certificate; do not assume today's bill fixes future contributions.

**Reserve planning:** Check whether the building has a repair reserve and how future works will be funded. Section 38ΚΗ allows a management fund and owner contributions; this guide does not establish a typical reserve or repair levy.

## How do I protect myself?

1. **Ask for the full cost breakdown in writing** before signing anything. Have your adviser reconcile it with the contract and provider quotations.
2. **Build a property-specific budget** using the applicable VAT regime, fees and written quotations.
3. **Read the common expenses clause** in the contract. Ask for the current monthly fee AND the management company's financial report.
4. **Compare FX quotations** by the net amount received and all fees.
5. **Obtain independent legal advice** and a written fee quotation.
6. **Check the title deed status** before buying resale — obtain the relevant Land Registry certificates and have a lawyer review title, encumbrances and the contract. Ask your adviser to assess risks beyond the registry entries.
7. **Calculate the true VAT** — use the [Tax Department's calculator](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tfa/en/documents/vat-5-calculation-tool-for-the-purchase-building-of-a-house/) with your actual property dimensions, not the agent's "5% VAT" claim.
