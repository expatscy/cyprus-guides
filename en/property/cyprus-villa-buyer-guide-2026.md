> **Short answer:** Check the actual house, land rights, title, permits, taxes and running costs. A marketing description, district average or residence-investment price does not replace property-specific due diligence.

## What should I check when a listing says villa?

Ask what is actually being sold: the registered land parcel, the house, any shared rights and the title situation. Do not assume the word **villa** guarantees a separate plot title, a particular setback or permission for a swimming pool.

The [DLS buyer brochure](https://portal.dls.moi.gov.cy/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Basic-Information-before-the-Purchase-of-Immovable-Property.pdf) recommends checking ownership, encumbrances, planning/building permits and the registration requirements. For an unfinished or undivided unit, the required completion and building-authority certificates must exist before separate registration.

Ask an independent lawyer and qualified engineer to check:

- The registered property and land rights, boundaries, access and any shared property.
- The applicable zone, density, coverage, setbacks and the actual approved plans.
- Whether the house, pool, extensions and outbuildings match the permits.
- Separate title status, mortgages, prohibitions and any other encumbrances.
- The seller’s obligations for completion, certificates and transfer.

Use our [building-permit guide](/en/property/building-permits-cyprus-coefficients/) for the distinction between density, coverage and the property’s applicable rules.

## How should I compare villa prices by region?

Request dated quotations and compare the same property type, floor-area definition, condition, land rights, exact location and tax treatment. Check whether parking, appliances, pool equipment and finishes are included.

The [Central Bank Q4 2025 RPPI report](https://www.centralbank.cy/images/media/pdf/RPPI_2025Q4_ENG.pdf) reports national annual growth of **3.4% for houses**, **9.6% for apartments** and **7.1% for the combined residential index**, versus Q4 2024. The house series is not a separate villa-price series. These are historical changes, not current EUR/m² quotations or future return promises.

For a dated comparison of the combined index across the five districts, read our [district guide](/en/property/cyprus-property-prices-by-district-2026/). Browse [property listings](https://research.cy/search/) and confirm each quotation with its seller.

## How should I budget for running costs?

Request bills and written service quotations for the actual property. Include electricity, water, pool care, garden maintenance, insurance, local charges and any management/common-property obligations. Check who pays for private roads and shared facilities before signing.

**One dated supplier estimate:** in its **6 April 2026** Paphos guide, [The Pool People](https://www.thepoolpeople.eu/uncategorized/paphos-pool-maintenance-cost-2026-pricing-guide-for-property-owners/) FAQ quotes **EUR 90–150/month** for a standard **8×4 m residential pool**, and a separate **EUR 25–40/month** chemical budget. Those inputs give **EUR 1,380–2,280 for twelve months** if both apply throughout the year. This is that provider’s published estimate, not an island-wide tariff or a quote for your pool. Its descriptions of chemical inclusion differ across the page; get the included services, visit schedule, VAT and chemicals confirmed in writing. Electricity, water and repairs are additional budget items.

Do not substitute a generic villa size for actual consumption or a universal annual maintenance bill.

## What purchase taxes and fees apply?

### VAT

The standard VAT rate is **19%**. A taxable new dwelling is different from an ordinarily VAT-exempt occupied residential resale; confirm the transaction rather than relying on the words new build or resale.

For the **ordinary reduced-rate scheme**, the buyer is an **individual aged 18+**, using the dwelling as their **main and permanent residence in Cyprus**, with the other statutory conditions:

- **5%** on the qualifying first **130 m²**, with a **EUR 350,000 reduced-rate value cap**.
- Total area no more than **190 m²** and total value no more than **EUR 475,000**.
- Area/value allocation determines the amount at 5%; it is not necessarily the full price. Disability and large-family provisions have different conditions.

Changing qualifying use within **ten years** can trigger repayment of the actual original VAT saving proportionate to the remaining period. Notify and pay within **30 days**, subject to statutory death and eligible-child transfer exceptions.

The transition concerns a planning permission or application submitted **by 31 October 2023**, under the statutory old-scheme conditions. The **15 June 2026** declaration deadline applied to building permits issued **by 31 December 2024**. The Commissioner may examine qualifying delayed cases through **31 December 2026** where planning-authority delays prevented timely completion; this is a conditional discretion, not a universal extension for every later permit. Read the [property-tax guide](/en/property/property-taxes-cyprus-vat-cgt/) before relying on a transition.

### Transfer fees and stamp duty

DLS ordinary transfer-fee bands are:

| Value band | Rate |
|------------|------|
| First EUR 85,000 | 3% |
| Next EUR 85,000, up to EUR 170,000 | 5% |
| Above EUR 170,000 | 8% |

The ordinary **50% discount** has exceptions, including the specified foreclosure acquisition cases. Transfer fees are exempt where VAT applies to the **same property transaction**; not every listing described as new qualifies merely because of its label. For a hypothetical **EUR 300,000** sole-buyer purchase receiving the ordinary discount, the fee is **EUR 8,600**.

Stamp duty was repealed from **1 January 2026**. Documents signed by **any party before that date** retain the earlier rules.

### Annual charges and a later sale

National **Immovable Property Tax ceased from 2017**. Obtain the current local-authority, sewerage and common-expense charges for the actual property; this abolition does not eliminate them.

Cyprus capital-gains tax is generally **20%** on taxable gains from Cyprus immovable property, with statutory deductions and exemptions. From **1 January 2026**, the qualifying primary-residence lifetime allowance is **EUR 150,000**, subject to conditions including owner-occupation, normally **five years** (or **ten years** for a subsequent qualifying exemption) and the **1,500 m²** land limit. It is not an automatic deduction for every villa sale. The general **EUR 30,000** and qualifying farming **EUR 50,000** allowances are not added on top of the residence ceiling. See the [property-tax guide](/en/property/property-taxes-cyprus-vat-cgt/) for the full conditions.

## What contract and title protections should I arrange?

Have a lawyer inspect the seller’s title and a current Search Certificate. For applicable contracts after the 2023 amendment, the seller must include a Search Certificate dated within **five working days of signing** as an integral part of the contract.

The ordinary sale-contract deposit deadline is **six months from signing**, subject to statutory conditions and exceptions. Accepted deposit creates an encumbrance with priority from deposit; it does not automatically erase prior mortgages or guarantee completion. DLS lists a **EUR 50 contract-deposit fee**. Specific mortgage-release protections require the applicable statutory procedure.

DLS advises against paying the full price before title transfer. Agree completion, permitted works, payments and transfer obligations with professional advice; do not assume a standard escrow arrangement resolves every title problem.

## What if I want to rent the villa to visitors?

Use property-specific rental and expense assumptions. **Gross yield = annual rent ÷ purchase price × 100**: hypothetical **EUR 20,000** annual rent on a **EUR 400,000** purchase gives **5%**, before expenses and additional acquisition costs. This does not establish achievable rent, occupied weeks or net return.

For self-service tourist accommodation, the [Deputy Ministry’s guidance](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/26/2024/05/SELF_SERVICE_ESTABLISHMENTS_2024_en.pdf) requires registration and a special operating label/number before advertising or renting. The number must appear in promotional material and relevant transactions. Registration includes tax-registration, insurance and supporting-document conditions; check the current application checklist before operating.

The initial permit is valid **three years**. Renewal applications must be submitted within the **three months before expiry**; a renewal is valid **five years**. The [Ministry’s renewal notice](https://www.gov.cy/en/tourism/renewing-the-registration-license-for-self-service-establishments/) states a **EUR 222** fee in all cases; late renewal requires a new initial-registration application and its documents, with a three-year permit.

## Can buying a villa support permanent residence?

A qualifying residential investment may support an application under **Regulation 6(2)**, separate from ordinary **Category F**. The current policy normally requires a developer’s first sale of a house/apartment, at least **EUR 300,000 excluding VAT paid**, and documented funds from abroad. Historical pre-**7 May 2013** residential resale and payment-account exceptions have specific conditions.

Secure annual **foreign income** must be at least **EUR 50,000**, plus **EUR 15,000 for a spouse** and **EUR 10,000 per minor**. A tax return or an independent certified accountant’s official certification may evidence it. Criminal-record, public-order, accommodation, insurance and other criteria also apply.

Spouse and children under **18** may be included; adult dependent children have separate conditional rules. There is **no general employment right**, with specific director exceptions. The policy’s **two-month processing estimate** concerns a complete qualifying application; it is not an approval guarantee.

Maintain the investment and continuing conditions. The **17 May 2023 clarification** removed annual income-proof submission, requires criminal-record certificates every **three years**, and retains annual investment evidence and insurance evidence where not GESY beneficiaries. Check the current investor-route supporting-document checklist rather than assuming the separate Categories A–F medical announcement determines this route.

Read the [official policy and clarifications](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/documents/etaireies-ependytes-monimi-diamoni/adeies-metanasteysis-ependyton/) and obtain advice before choosing a property for residence. A purchase does not automatically grant residence or citizenship.

## Next steps

1. Compare actual houses against your own budget, travel and lifestyle requirements.
2. Have independent legal and technical advisers check title, land rights, permits and the contract.
3. Obtain written tax treatment, transaction-cost calculations and running-cost quotations.
4. If renting, confirm the operating requirements and test lower-rent and vacancy scenarios.
5. If applying for residence, verify the full route-specific criteria before committing funds.
