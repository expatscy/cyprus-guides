> **Short answer:** Permission to acquire property, registered ownership and immigration residence are separate matters. Check which foreign-buyer permission rules apply, the property’s legal status, the contract and the actual VAT treatment before paying.

This guide focuses on the foreign-buyer permission regime. For the wider process, costs and a dated market-index snapshot, see our [property buying guide](/en/property/buying-property-cyprus-complete-guide-2026/).

## Who can buy property in Cyprus?

**Cap. 109** excludes EU/EEA citizens from its foreign-buyer definition. Foreign companies and foreign-controlled companies can fall within the regime; incorporation in Cyprus alone does not establish an exemption. Check the statutory definition and your circumstances.

Current Ministry guidance permits:

- A building plot or undivided land up to **4,000 m²** for an owner-occupied dwelling; or
- Up to **two qualifying units**: two dwellings, or a dwelling plus a shop up to **100 m²**, or a dwelling plus an office up to **250 m²**. The restriction applies jointly to couples.

### Permission application

Use **COMM 145** with the required documents through the **District Administration where the property is located**. The Ministry states **no application fee** and a **two-to-three-week processing estimate**; approval and timing are not guaranteed.

Under **Cap. 109, section 3**, an acquisition contract does not itself give a buyer within the regime the right to acquire ownership without the required permission. Obtain advice before signing or paying. Permission to buy does not itself confer immigration residence.

## What is the step-by-step buying process?

### 1. Signing the Sale Contract

Ask an independent lawyer to review the contract, title and permissions. For deposit under **Law 81(I)/2011, section 3**, the contract must be written, sufficiently identify the parties and property, state the consideration and be signed by all parties. Under **section 4(1A)**, for contracts concluded after the 2023 amendment took effect, the seller must include a Search Certificate dated within **five working days of signing** as an integral part of the contract.

Stamp duty was repealed from **1 January 2026** under **Law 239(I)/2025**. Documents signed by any party before that date retain the earlier stamp-duty rules.

### 2. Deposit at the Land Registry

The ordinary statutory deposit deadline is **six months from signing**, subject to section 3 conditions and exceptions. An accepted deposit creates an **encumbrance** with priority from the deposit date. Sections 3A and 5 provide additional mortgage-related protections and procedures; deposit does not automatically erase prior mortgages or guarantee completion. The current DLS procedure lists a **EUR 50 contract-deposit fee**; confirm the submission requirements for your file.

### 3. Obtaining the Title Deed

A certificate of registration confirms registration in the DLS Land Register. Check the applicable situation:

- **Existing separate title deed and no encumbrances:** check the DLS sale/exchange requirements, tax clearances, identity documents and applicable foreign-buyer permission. DLS advises against paying the full purchase price before title transfer
- **Signed sale contract but transfer postponed:** arrange deposit within the ordinary six-month deadline, subject to the statutory conditions and exceptions described above
- **New build or unit still under division:** a separate title deed cannot be issued until the required completion and building-authority certificates exist; require a contractual deadline for the seller to obtain permits, certificates, and the certificate of registration

## How does the reduced 5% VAT on first homes work?

The ordinary reduced-rate scheme concerns an **individual aged 18 or over**, using the dwelling as their **main and permanent residence in Cyprus**, with the other statutory eligibility conditions. It is not simply a first-purchase discount.

- First **130 m²** with a **EUR 350,000 reduced-rate value cap**.
- Total area must not exceed **190 m²** and total value must not exceed **EUR 475,000** for the ordinary scheme.
- Area/value allocation determines the part taxed at **5%**; the standard rate is **19%**. Disability and large-family provisions have separate conditions.
- Changing qualifying use within **ten years** can trigger repayment of the **actual original saving**, proportionate to the remaining period. Notify and pay within **30 days**; check the statutory death and eligible-child transfer exceptions.

Follow the current Tax Department application instructions and provide the required supporting documents. Confirm the approved tax treatment before budgeting or invoicing; company affiliations alone do not establish eligibility. See our [property-tax guide](/en/property/property-taxes-cyprus-vat-cgt/) for the transition and full conditions.

## How does permanent residence through property purchase work?

A qualifying investment may support an application under **Regulation 6(2)**. This is separate from ordinary **Category F**, and a purchase alone does not guarantee approval.

For the residential route, current policy normally requires a developer’s **first sale** of a house/apartment, with at least **EUR 300,000 excluding VAT** paid and documented funds from abroad. The policy includes a historical pre-7-May-2013 residential resale exception and specific applicant/spouse/company payment-account conditions.

Other requirements include:

- Secure annual **foreign income of EUR 50,000**, plus **EUR 15,000 for a spouse** and **EUR 10,000 for each minor**. A tax return or an independent certified accountant’s official certification may evidence it.
- Applicant/spouse clean criminal records from origin and residence countries where different, public-order/security criteria, suitable accommodation and health-insurance conditions.
- Spouse and children under **18** may be included; certain adult dependent children have separate conditional application rules.
- No general employment right: the applicant/spouse declare they do not intend employment, subject to the policy’s director exceptions. Obtain route-specific advice before changing an existing work/residence arrangement.

Maintain the qualifying investment and comply with continuing conditions. The **17 May 2023 clarification** removed annual income-proof submission, requires criminal-record certificates every **three years**, and retains annual investment evidence and insurance evidence where not GESY beneficiaries. Check the **current investor-route supporting-document checklist**, including any examination requirements, rather than assuming the separate Categories A–F announcement determines this route.

Read the [official investor policy and clarifications](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/documents/etaireies-ependytes-monimi-diamoni/adeies-metanasteysis-ependyton/) before selecting property for a residence application.

## What practical tips should I know?

### When choosing a property:

- Check for the seller's **title deed**
- Clarify the history of **permits** (planning, building)
- Ensure there are no **encumbrances** on the property

### When completing the transaction:

- Hire an **independent lawyer** (not from the developer)
- Deposit the contract at the **Land Registry** as early as possible
- Keep all payment documents to confirm the origin of funds

### When paying from abroad (GBP, USD, other currencies):

Compare provider quotations for the actual currency, amount and route: total fees, exchange rate, euros received and any intermediary-bank charges. Do not assume a universal bank or specialist-provider percentage. Keep records showing the source and route of the payment.

**British buyers:** UK nationality alone does not give EU/EEA citizenship. Check the applicable Cap. 109 permission requirement and obtain advice from a lawyer qualified for the Cyprus transaction; property permission is separate from immigration residence.
