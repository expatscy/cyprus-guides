> **Short answer:** Check foreign-buyer permission, the property’s legal and title status, the contract and the transaction’s tax treatment before paying. A property purchase and immigration permission are separate matters.

DLS recorded **18,114 deposited sale contracts in 2025**, compared with **15,797 in 2024** — an increase of approximately **15%**. These are registered contract counts, not a count of completed title transfers ([DLS dated 2 January 2026](https://portal.dls.moi.gov.cy/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Contracts-of-Sale-2024-2025.pdf)). For the permission process, see our [non-EU property buyer guide](/en/property/buying-property-cyprus/).

## Who can buy property in Cyprus?

### EU/EEA Citizens

EU/EEA citizens are excluded from the definition of foreign buyer under **Cap. 109, section 2**. This removes that permission requirement; ordinary property, planning, contract and tax rules still apply.

### Buyers within the foreign-buyer permission regime

The Ministry’s current guidance requires **COMM 145** and supporting documents through the **District Administration where the property is located**. It permits:

- A plot or undivided land up to **4,000 m²** to build an owner-occupied dwelling; or
- Up to **two units**: two dwellings, or one dwelling and a shop up to **100 m²**, or one dwelling and an office up to **250 m²**. The restriction applies jointly to couples.

The Ministry publishes **no application fee** and a **two-to-three-week processing estimate**, rather than a guaranteed completion date. Foreign companies and foreign-controlled companies can also fall within **Cap. 109**; incorporation in Cyprus alone does not establish an exemption.

Check permission requirements before committing. A sale contract or permission to buy property does not itself establish a right to reside in Cyprus.

## What is the purchase process step by step?

### Step 1: Find the Property and Agree on Terms

Search platforms, visit developments, and shortlist properties. Once you agree on a price with the seller or developer:

- Request a recent **title document and Search Certificate** from the seller; check registered ownership, encumbrances, mortgages and prohibitions
- For new builds, verify that the developer holds a valid **planning permit** and **building permit**
- Under **Law 81(I)/2011, section 4(1A)**, for contracts concluded after the 2023 amendment took effect, the seller must include a Search Certificate as an integral part of the contract, dated within **five working days of signing**

[Compare property listings on research.cy](https://research.cy).

### Step 2: Hire an Independent Lawyer

Obtain a written fee quotation, including any VAT and disbursements, and agree the work covered.

Ask your lawyer to cover:
- Draft or review the sale contract
- Conduct due diligence on the title deed and permits
- Handle the Land Registry deposit
- Manage any applicable foreign-buyer permission application

Ask an independent lawyer to advise on your interests and agree the scope of representation.

### Step 3: Sign the Sale Contract

For deposit under **Law 81(I)/2011, section 3**, the contract must be written, sufficiently identify the parties and property, state the consideration and be signed by all parties. Ask your lawyer to document the payment schedule, completion date, breach remedies and agreed off-plan specifications.

### Step 4: Deposit the Contract at the Land Registry

Under **Law 81(I)/2011**, an accepted deposit creates an **encumbrance** with priority from the deposit date. The ordinary deadline is **six months from signing**, subject to the section 3 conditions and exceptions; for example, the law addresses property not yet registered in a seller’s name. Sections 3A and 5 contain additional mortgage and priority protections and procedures. Deposit does not automatically erase prior mortgages or guarantee completion.

Stamp duty was repealed from **1 January 2026** under **Law 239(I)/2025**. Documents signed by any party before that date retain the earlier stamp-duty rules.

### Step 5: Pay and Complete

Follow the agreed payment and handover schedule after the necessary checks. DLS advises **not paying the full purchase price before transfer of the certificate of registration into your name**.

### Step 6: Transfer the Title Deed

Check the property’s separate registration, any applicable foreign-buyer permission, outstanding encumbrances and the DLS transfer requirements with your adviser. For a unit under construction, DLS explains that registration requires completion of works and the relevant certificates. A sale contract and registered ownership are different stages.

## What is the full costs breakdown?

Costs depend on the transaction and quotations. See [hidden costs of buying property](/en/property/hidden-costs-buying-property-cyprus/) and obtain a complete written budget.

| Item | What to check |
|---|---|
| Legal work, survey and valuation | Written quotations, VAT and disbursements |
| VAT | Whether the supply is taxable and whether the buyer/property qualifies for 5%; the standard rate is 19% |
| Transfer fees | Progressive bands, VAT exemption, ordinary discount and its foreclosure exception |
| Stamp duty | Repealed from 1 January 2026; pre-2026 signing exception |
| Search certificates | Required certificate type and applicable DLS charge |
| Financing | The actual lender’s arrangement, valuation, insurance and other charges |
| Foreign-buyer permission | Ministry guidance states no application fee |

**Conditional tax example:** For an otherwise fully eligible **100 m² new dwelling priced at EUR 300,000 excluding VAT**, 5% VAT on the whole price is **EUR 15,000**. If the full price instead attracts 19%, VAT is **EUR 57,000**. For an ordinary VAT-free EUR 300,000 transfer to one buyer, with the 50% discount and no foreclosure exception, transfer fees are **EUR 8,600**. These examples exclude professional and financing costs and do not establish a market-wide total-cost percentage.

### VAT: 5% vs 19%

For the ordinary reduced-rate scheme, an individual aged **18 or over** must use the dwelling as their **main and permanent residence in Cyprus** and satisfy the other statutory requirements:

- The reduced rate concerns the first **130 m²**, with a **EUR 350,000 reduced-rate value cap**.
- Total area must not exceed **190 m²** and total value must not exceed **EUR 475,000**; if either overall limit is exceeded, the ordinary scheme does not apply.
- Area/value allocation determines the part at 5%; it is not automatically 5% on the full price. Statutory disability and large-family provisions require separate checking.
- Changing the qualifying use within **ten years** can trigger repayment of the **actual original VAT saving**, proportionate to the remaining period. Notify and pay within **30 days**; statutory death and eligible-child transfer exceptions must be checked.

**Transition:** The old first-200-m² rules may apply where planning permission was obtained or the planning application submitted **by 31 October 2023**, subject to the transitional conditions. The Tax Department’s 4 May 2026 notice gives **15 June 2026** for declarations concerning building permits issued by **31 December 2024**. Under VAT Act section 63, the Commissioner may examine qualifying delayed declarations through **31 December 2026** where planning-authority delays prevented timely completion; this is a discretionary provision, not an unconditional extension for every project.

Ordinary occupied-home resale is generally outside VAT, but verify the actual supply; the new-build/resale label alone is not a tax determination.

### Transfer Fees

The ordinary progressive scale is reduced by **50%**. Check the exception for a seller’s acquisition through the statutory foreclosure procedures:

| Property Value | Rate | Discounted Rate |
|---------------|------|----------------|
| First EUR 85,000 | 3% | 1.5% |
| EUR 85,001-170,000 | 5% | 2.5% |
| Above EUR 170,000 | 8% | 4% |

Transfer fees are **exempt where VAT applies to the same transaction/property**. The exemption is not determined by the marketing label alone.

## What is the reality of title deeds in Cyprus?

DLS explains that a certificate of registration establishes the property’s registration in the Land Register. Ask for a recent document and investigate the reason if a completed unit lacks its own title. For a unit under development, check the necessary permits, completion certificates, division arrangements and the contractual timetable for separate registration.

A new unit under construction cannot yet have its own certificate of registration. DLS states that registration requires a completion-of-works certificate from the supervising engineer and a certificate from the competent building authority. Do not assume a universal one-to-three-year title-issuance period.

## How does permanent residence through property work?

A qualifying investment may support an application under **Regulation 6(2)**. Ordinary **Category F** is a separate route; a purchase alone does not guarantee approval. For the residential investment route, the current policy includes:

| Requirement | Residential-route conditions |
|---|---|
| Property | Normally a house/apartment bought from a developer as a first sale, at least EUR 300,000 excluding VAT; the policy has a historical pre-7-May-2013 resale exception |
| Payment | At least EUR 300,000 excluding VAT paid with documented funds from abroad; the policy and clarifications specify eligible applicant/spouse/company accounts and payment to the seller in Cyprus |
| Income | Secure annual foreign income at least EUR 50,000, plus EUR 15,000 for a spouse and EUR 10,000 for each minor; tax return or an independent certified accountant’s official certification can evidence it |
| Other criteria | Applicant/spouse clean criminal records from origin and residence countries where different, public-order/security criteria, suitable accommodation and health-insurance requirements |
| Employment | No general employment right: applicant/spouse declare they do not intend employment, subject to the policy’s director exceptions |
| Family | Spouse and children under 18 can be included; certain adult dependent children have separate conditional application rules |
| Timing | Department estimate approximately two months from a complete application, not guaranteed |

Maintain the qualifying investment and comply with the policy’s continuing conditions. The 17 May 2023 clarification removed annual income-proof submission, requires criminal-record certificates every three years, and retains annual investment evidence and insurance evidence where not GESY beneficiaries. See the [full official policy and clarifications](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/documents/etaireies-ependytes-monimi-diamoni/adeies-metanasteysis-ependyton/) before choosing a property for residence purposes. Confirm the **current investor-route supporting-document checklist**, including any examination requirements, before applying; do not infer that the separate Categories A–F medical announcement determines the Regulation 6(2) checklist.

## What is a realistic timeline?

Get a case-specific schedule covering the legal checks, permission application, contract deposit, financing, construction/handover and separate title transfer. Confirm the responsible party and remedy for each deadline in the contract. Neither a standard online timeline nor permission to buy establishes when a unit will be completed or when residence permission will be granted.

## What 2026 legislative changes should I watch?

- **Stamp duty repeal:** effective 1 January 2026, with the pre-2026 signing exception described above.
- **Reduced-VAT transition:** the ordinary 15 June 2026 declaration deadline has passed; the delayed-case discretion through 31 December 2026 is conditional.
- **Corporate income tax:** the rate is **15% from tax year 2026**, previously 12.5%; buying through a company requires separate tax analysis.
- **Indirect property-company shares:** the CGT threshold was reduced from **50% to 20%** from 1 January 2026. The test concerns market value derived from Cyprus immovable property and applies to indirect holdings; directly property-owning company shares do not need that threshold test. Statutory exemptions also require checking.

Compare actual asking prices and recent suitable comparables for the area and property type. The CBC index measures price changes; it is not a district average asking price per square metre.

## What practical tips should I know?

1. **Check title and encumbrances before signing.** Request the seller’s recent Search Certificate and title document.
2. **Arrange contract deposit with your lawyer.** Confirm the applicable statutory deadline and any prior mortgages.
3. **Confirm VAT treatment before budgeting.** Check the actual area/value allocation and the buyer’s eligibility.
4. **Get quotations and the full cost schedule.** Include professional, financing and ongoing property expenses.
5. **Arrange an independent condition inspection** where appropriate.
6. **Keep payment records.** Investment-residence applications require evidence of the qualifying payment and funds from abroad.
