Use district data to understand past price movement, then compare individual properties using current quotations. Keep asking prices, completed transactions and rental assumptions separate when preparing a budget.

## What does the official district snapshot show?

The [Central Bank of Cyprus Q4 2025 RPPI report](https://www.centralbank.cy/images/media/pdf/RPPI_2025Q4_ENG.pdf) reports the following **year-on-year changes from Q4 2024 to Q4 2025**, for its **combined house-and-apartment index**:

| District | Annual index change |
|----------|---------------------|
| Limassol | +9.9% |
| Larnaca | +8.3% |
| Paphos | +7.6% |
| Nicosia | +1.0% |
| Famagusta | Unchanged |

Source: report section 2 and Table 5. This is a dated snapshot, not a forecast for 2026.

The RPPI uses market valuations and statistical methodology. It measures price changes; these percentages do not give a current asking price per square metre, a purchase budget or a rental yield. Do not use them to rank districts by absolute affordability.

## How should I compare actual purchase prices?

Build a shortlist in each district you are considering and request a dated written quotation. Compare:

- Property type, condition, bedrooms and the same definition of floor area.
- Exact location and the travel time you personally need.
- Whether the quotation includes VAT, parking, storage and any optional finishes.
- Separate title status, permits, encumbrances and the seller’s delivery commitments.
- Total purchase costs, rather than the headline price alone.

For document checks, the [DLS buyer brochure](https://portal.dls.moi.gov.cy/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Basic-Information-before-the-Purchase-of-Immovable-Property.pdf) recommends checking the seller’s registration and property encumbrances, permits and registration requirements. Read our [buying guide](/en/property/buying-property-cyprus-complete-guide-2026/) and [property-tax guide](/en/property/property-taxes-cyprus-vat-cgt/) before budgeting.

[Browse property listings on research.cy](https://research.cy/search/) and confirm the price and terms with the seller. Treat a listing as a quotation to investigate, rather than proof of a district average.

## How should I compare rental returns?

Use a property-specific rental scenario instead of a universal district yield. Request evidence for the proposed rent and occupied months; budget separately for vacancy, management, common expenses, maintenance, insurance and applicable taxes.

**Gross yield = annual rent ÷ purchase price × 100.** For a hypothetical purchase at **EUR 200,000** and annual rent of **EUR 12,000**, gross yield is **6%**. Expenses reduce the amount retained; including additional acquisition costs in the investment denominator also changes the return. The example does not establish market rent or occupancy.

## How should I choose a district?

Start with your own requirements: employment location, school or healthcare access, coastal or inland preference, intended rental use and the amount you can spend after transaction costs. Visit your shortlisted neighbourhoods at different times and check services and journey times yourself.

For an investment, test the result with lower rent, vacancy and higher expenses. For a residence application, check the actual immigration-route conditions before committing to property; a price alone does not establish eligibility. See the [non-EU buying guide](/en/property/buying-property-cyprus/) for the distinction between buying permission and investor residence.

Do not make a purchase depend on an unconfirmed redevelopment timetable or predicted appreciation. Check the current official project position and your contract before treating future works as a reason to pay more.
