> **Short answer:** Cyprus has distinct housing schemes with different beneficiaries, amounts, property conditions and application windows. Identify the actual scheme and current call before budgeting a grant; a past announcement is not an open application window.

## What happened to the young-buyer scheme?

The [Ministry’s second call for 2025](https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/documents/housing-subsidy-scheme-for-young-couples-and-or-individuals-up-to-41-years-old/) is titled “Housing Subsidy Scheme for Young Couples and/or Individuals up to 41 Years Old” and concerns housing with a maximum area of **150 m²** in specified areas. Applications were accepted from **15 January 2025 at noon to 30 November 2025 at noon**. Those advertised dates have passed; check for a later call before applying.

On **4 May 2026**, the [Ministry announced additional funding](https://www.gov.cy/esoterika-themata/parachorisi-prosthetis-epidotisis-e11-ekat-gia-stirixi-epiprostheton-277-neon-kai-nearon-zevgarion/) of approximately **EUR 11 million** for another **277** applicants who had already applied and qualified. It describes assistance **up to EUR 50,000**, based on income and household composition. This announcement concerns existing applications, not a new application window.

For any new call, obtain its Announcement and Implementation Guide before relying on age, citizenship, residence, income, property-ownership or contribution conditions. Submit using the method specified in that call; the Funding Programmes Portal is a place to find programme information, not a universal application form.

## What was the mortgage interest subsidy?

The [Ministry of Finance’s 2025 call](https://www.gov.cy/mof/en/interest-rate-subsidy-scheme-for-income-qualified-home-loans/) advertised applications from **2 January to 31 March 2025**, in hard copy through the **KEP/KEPO citizen-service centres**. Treat those dates as historical and check the Treasury for any new call.

Its stated criteria include all of the following:

- A loan agreement signed from **1 January 2022 to 31 December 2023**, with a loan account opened.
- A performing floating-rate home loan, not taken to repay or restructure a prior loan.
- A loan no more than **EUR 400,000** and annual family income no more than **EUR 50,000**.
- Tax obligations through **tax year 2023** settled or regulated.

The scheme subsidised **50% of the average interest-rate increase** relative to the rate at signing, for **two years from first disbursement**, capped at **two percentage points**. It is not a general subsidy for every existing mortgage.

## What is the Renovate-Rent call?

The [Ministry’s second Renovate-Rent call](https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/documents/housing-scheme-renovate-rent/) provides grants to qualifying owners to renovate or upgrade vacant, inactive or unused homes for affordable letting to eligible tenants with moderate or low incomes.

The advertised **owner application window is 16 March 2026 to 31 December 2027**. Tenant applications are advertised from **16 March 2026**. Applications use **[prositistegi.cy](https://www.prositistegi.cy/)** and the call’s prescribed forms and instructions. The Ministry reserves the ability to extend or withdraw the call; verify its current position before submission.

Check the full owner and tenant guides for amounts, property conditions, rent and continuing obligations. Do not carry eligibility rules across from the young-buyer or state-plot scheme.

## What changed in rural housing assistance?

The [Ministry’s rural scheme page](https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/documents/housing-scheme-for-the-revitalization-of-specific-rural-areas/) states a **2 March 2026** start date.

The [Presidency’s 11 March 2026 revision summary](https://www.diakivernisi.gov.cy/gr/epikairotita/1-enisxyetai-oysiastika-i-stirixi-gia-katoikia-stin-ypaithro) describes these maxima for the **rural revitalisation scheme**:

| Household | Maximum assistance stated |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Single person or household of up to three | EUR 20,000 |
| Household of four or more | EUR 25,000 |

It also describes a **20% increase in the maximum for displaced applicants**, with the applicable eligibility rules. The separate mountainous, remote and disadvantaged-area provisions include other categories and special-construction support; these are not one universal EUR 20,000 programme.

Use the current guide and designated-area list to check eligibility, income, acquisition/expense dates, other property and how your household is assessed. A stated maximum is not a guaranteed grant.

## What is the 2026 state-plot scheme?

The [Ministry’s scheme page published 18 September 2026](https://www.gov.cy/moi/documents/nea-eniaia-stegastiki-politiki/schedio-diathesis-oikopedon-se-oikogeneies-me-vasi-eisodimatikon-kritirion/) concerns state plots for an owner-occupied home, subject to income and other conditions.

- Beneficiaries are **couples with or without children and single-parent families**; single individuals are excluded.
- **Cypriot citizenship** is required, for at least one partner in a couple. For a couple, at least one partner must **not have reached 45** when applying.
- The community/residence connection includes the relevant community or a neighbouring community within **15 km**, with the scheme’s origin and priority provisions.
- The property test covers the applicant, spouse/partner, children and parents; residentially usable property and disposals within the specified **three-year** period can affect eligibility.

The published gross annual household income limits are:

| Household | Maximum gross annual income |
|-----------|-----------------------------|
| Couple or single-parent family | EUR 45,000 |
| Three persons | EUR 50,000 |
| Four persons | EUR 55,000 |
| Five or more persons | EUR 65,000 |

The price is **25% of the plot’s general-assessment value after subdivision**, payable in **one instalment when signing**. It is not a universal offer at 25% of any advertised market price. The scheme states that the state bears subdivision and infrastructure costs under its provisions, and that the completed payment accompanies titling.

The home must not exceed **150 m² total buildable area**. Construction must be completed within **three years of the agreement**; the District Administration may approve a justified **one-year extension**. The plot cannot be transferred before home completion, and the home cannot be sold or disposed of before **five years after completion**. Non-compliance can trigger the scheme’s return, transfer or remaining-value remedies.

Apply under the **District Administration’s actual notice** for available plots and its deadline. Allocation follows the scheme’s priority and assessment rules, with a lottery where approved beneficiaries exceed available plots. Read the full guide and application documents before assuming that publication alone makes a plot available to you.

## How should I check eligibility and purchase costs?

For the actual call, check the household definition, citizenship/residence, ages, income year, other property, eligible location and expenses, submission method, deadlines and continuing obligations. Obtain confirmation from the responsible scheme officer before committing to a purchase based on expected assistance.

Reduced VAT has separate statutory conditions: the ordinary individual main/permanent-residence scheme requires **age 18+**, a qualifying first **130 m²** at **5%** with a **EUR 350,000 reduced-rate value cap**, and total caps of **190 m²/EUR 475,000**. Area/value allocation and other eligibility conditions apply. See the [property-tax guide](/en/property/property-taxes-cyprus-vat-cgt/) before treating a grant as proof of VAT entitlement.

DLS transfer fees use **3%, 5% and 8% value bands**, normally with a **50% discount** and statutory exceptions. They are exempt where VAT applies to the **same property transaction**. Stamp duty was repealed from **1 January 2026**, while documents signed by **any party before that date** retain the earlier rules. These purchase rules are distinct from grant approval.

Request written legal, valuation, survey, renovation and utility quotations for the actual property. Use our [buying guide](/en/property/buying-property-cyprus-complete-guide-2026/) and [hidden-cost guide](/en/property/hidden-costs-buying-property-cyprus/) to prepare the budget; avoid assuming a universal professional-fee range or renovation percentage.

[Browse property listings](https://research.cy/search/) and verify the title, permits, encumbrances and any outstanding charges with independent advisers before signing.
