## Dmitry Motorov’s relocation experience

The publisher introduces **Dmitry Motorov** as an entrepreneur discussing his move and life in Cyprus. The video was uploaded on **7 November 2024** and runs **42 minutes 31 seconds**.

In the published captions, Dmitry says he had lived in Cyprus for **six years at the time of the recording**. He describes being born in Leningrad, growing up in St Petersburg, working online and wanting a milder climate for his family. He recalls stays on Samui and considering Marbella, then choosing Cyprus after a visit in May 2018 and making friends there.

He says **95% of his own communication** is in Russian. That is his personal estimate, rather than a figure for the island’s population or language use.

## Healthcare and education in his account

Dmitry estimates his family’s additional insurance at **about EUR 3,000 a year** at the time of the interview. He describes choosing **American Academy** after visiting schools and preferring its atmosphere for his children. These are his family’s reported experience and choices, rather than a current insurance quotation or an assessment of every school.

**Current healthcare note:** The [HIO service list](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hioghshealthcareservices) includes **inpatient care** provided by contracted providers. Check [beneficiary eligibility](https://www.gesy.org.cy/en-us/hiobeneficiaryeligiblility), the particular covered service and its access conditions. The interview’s insurance discussion does not establish a blanket exclusion for expensive operations. See our [GeSY/private-care comparison](/en/life/gesy-vs-private-healthcare-cyprus/) and [school comparison](/en/life/schools-cyprus-comparison/) for further planning.

## Tax discussion and current guidance

The publisher lists a chapter on tax benefits and IT companies. Its date matters when assessing the discussion.

**Current non-dom note:** The [Tax Department’s guidance](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/tax-residency-domicility/) concerns **domicile and tax-residence history**. Birthplace alone does not establish entitlement. Deemed domicile applies after Cyprus tax residence in at least **17 of the 20 years immediately preceding the tax year**. Non-dom concerns **SDC**; check income tax, GESY and any other applicable liabilities separately. See our [non-dom guide](/en/business/non-dom-status-cyprus-60-day-rule/) before using the interview’s tax discussion for a decision.
