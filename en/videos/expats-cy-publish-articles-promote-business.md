Want to share your relocation experience, start a blog or introduce your business to people who need your services? I invite you to **expats.cy, an encyclopedia and social platform for expats worldwide**.

Cyprus is where the project began, and the examples in this video focus on Cyprus. You can join, share your experience and introduce your work from any country.

[Watch Katerina’s invitation on YouTube](https://youtu.be/rXeVOuF52as).

## Why I created expats.cy

When I moved to Cyprus, I needed practical answers about documents, schools and bank accounts. Information disappeared in long chat threads or became outdated. Repeated questions annoyed participants, and I was once banned for asking a question.

I started collecting useful information in one place. The original idea was a reference guide where people could find answers without scrolling through endless messages. But readers also wanted to share stories, discuss solutions and preserve their own experience. That is how the guide began becoming a social platform.

## Publish articles and share your experience

Your experience can save someone time and stress. You may have dealt with paperwork, chosen a school, found a suitable neighbourhood or learned something useful about everyday life abroad.

You can submit personal stories and useful articles for free. [Write an article](/article/new) and send it for editorial review. Include the country, city and date, explain the problem you faced and describe what worked.

## Introduce your business to expats

People moving abroad need lawyers, translators, teachers, property specialists, repair services and many other professionals. If you serve this audience, explain how your business can help.

**Personal contributions and commercial placements have different terms.** See [business publishing](/for-business/) and [service directory](/articles/directory). To discuss a suitable format, [contact me](/en/contact/) with your country, city, services and website.

If you publish expert articles on vc.ru, you can try a similar approach here: answer a potential client’s question, explain your process and show when your service is useful. A translator might explain how to prepare documents; a school might describe how it helps children adjust after relocation. State where you work and how readers can contact you.

## What the analytics in the video show

In the recording, I open Umami and discuss almost 8,000 visitors over the displayed 90 days and about 3,000 in a month. These figures refer to the period shown, not a promise of views or leads for any article or listing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I publish on expats.cy if I do not live in Cyprus?

Yes. Cyprus is the starting point, but the platform welcomes expats and professionals from any country. Specify the country and city your experience or service relates to.

### Can I publish personal articles for free?

Yes. You can submit personal stories and useful experience for free. Articles undergo editorial review.

### How can I list my business on expats.cy?

Check the business listing plans or email info@expats.cy with your country, city, services and website. Commercial placements have separate terms.
