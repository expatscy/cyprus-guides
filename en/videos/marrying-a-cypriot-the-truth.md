## Marriage to a Cypriot: A Personal Account

The publisher describes this interview as a guest’s account of meeting her husband, moving to Cyprus, family expectations and divorce. Her experiences are personal testimony, rather than evidence about every Cypriot family.

### Questions to Discuss as a Couple

- What role will each family play in your relationship?
- Which traditions matter to each of you?
- What are your expectations about family roles and language?

### Citizenship and Paperwork

Marriage does not automatically grant citizenship. The [Ministry of Interior’s M125 guidance](https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/documents/acquisition-of-cypriot-citizenship-due-to-marriage-civil-partnership-with-a-cypriot-citizen-form-m125/) explains the application route for qualifying spouses and civil partners. Check the applicable conditions and documents before applying.

For related topics, see our [marriage and divorce guide](/en/life/marriage-divorce-cyprus/) and [citizenship guide](/en/immigration/cyprus-citizenship-naturalization/).

### Advice

- Meet the family before the wedding
- Discuss expectations and plans
- Find a balance between cultures
- Preserve your identity
