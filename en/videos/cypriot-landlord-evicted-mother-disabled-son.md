## What this interview covers

The publisher introduces Lilya and her account of a rental dispute after moving to Cyprus with her children. In the interview she describes her older child as having a disability and says noise affected his sleep. The discussion covers communication, the agreement, electricity loss, moving and the deposit.

The video was uploaded on **8 December 2025** and lasts **23 minutes**. The amounts below describe her reported tenancy, rather than a current market-price estimate.

## Lilya’s account

- **Arrival and noise:** At **02:36–04:00**, she says she arrived in October with her children, rented an apartment and found the noise disturbed her older child’s sleep.
- **Communication:** At **04:16–05:45**, she describes asking the owner about alternatives or lower rent, using WhatsApp and a translator, and receiving a reply that she could move out. Her account distinguishes that discussion from formal termination notice.
- **Rent and deposit:** At **06:05–06:45**, she reports **EUR 1,000 including utilities** and a **EUR 1,900 deposit**. At **06:45–07:01**, she says she had not thoroughly read or translated the agreement before signing.
- **Electricity and advice:** At **10:55–11:10**, she says the electricity stopped during a period she had paid for and describes a prior message threatening disconnection. At **13:50–14:04**, she says she went to the police and was directed to a lawyer. The interview does not independently establish the mechanism of disconnection or the legal outcome.
- **Moving and handover:** At **14:55–15:58**, she says she found another apartment, received money and moving help from her employer, photographed and filmed the former apartment, and retained the keys on her lawyer’s advice pending a signed condition record. That reported advice concerns her situation; do not assume it applies to another tenancy.

## A note on legal applicability

The [Rent Control Law, section 2](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/1983_1_23/full.html), defines the property, tenancy and tenant categories to which that regime applies. Its property definition includes a building rented for a residence or shop, in a controlled area, completed by **31 December 1999**. Its definition of tenancy also contains exclusions, including furnished residential lettings shorter than **six months**.

These are part of the statutory conditions. Ask a Cyprus lawyer to assess the full definitions, your circumstances and agreement before relying on that regime, choosing a court or assuming a particular remedy. The interview does not establish a universal eviction timetable, entitlement to compensation or a finding of discrimination.

## Questions before signing or leaving

Have the agreement explained in a language you understand. Ask who pays utilities, how the deposit and deductions work, what notice and early-termination terms apply, and how condition and key handover will be recorded. Keep the signed agreement, payment records, correspondence and dated photographs.

For an existing dispute, obtain advice before deciding to withhold rent, use the deposit as rent, retain keys or leave. Use our [renting guide](/en/property/renting-property-cyprus/) and [scam-awareness guide](/en/life/scams-fraud-cyprus/) to prepare questions for the adviser.
