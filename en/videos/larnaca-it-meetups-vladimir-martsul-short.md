Looking for people with shared interests is one part of settling into life as an expat in Larnaca. In this short interview, published on 11 July 2024, developer Vladimir Martsul explains how he began bringing IT people together locally.

This is the short conversation about starting the community. The [longer Larnaca IT interview](/en/videos/larnaca-it-community-vladimir-martsul/) goes into its history, venues and the smaller groups that grew from it.

## A simple invitation instead of a large conference

Vladimir used to travel to Limassol to meet people and see its events. He and a colleague then asked a simpler question: why not invite people in Larnaca to sit by the beach, have a coffee or a beer and talk?

The early setting was Mackenzie beach, where aircraft pass low overhead. The idea was an informal meeting with common interests, rather than a highly produced conference. Vladimir describes himself as an introvert, and says another introvert’s story on Habr helped inspire him.

## Trying ideas without losing the focus

Katerina asks about lightweight meetups where an ordinary developer could give a talk. Vladimir is open to trying ideas, but the basic attraction remains meeting people, exchanging contacts and talking in a comfortable place.

He does not want to turn the group into an organisation for everyone in Larnaca. Keeping IT and related interests at its centre helps make the invitation clear and keeps the project close to what he enjoys.

## How people found the group

Vladimir mentions announcements on social media, reminders to friends and word of mouth. He is open to sponsorship, but does not want to spend his own money on advertising. That is an account of his position during the interview, not an announcement of an available sponsorship package.
