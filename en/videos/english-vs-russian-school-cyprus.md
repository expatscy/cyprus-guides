## Questions for your own decision

Visit your shortlisted schools and discuss the child’s needs. Ask about teaching language, curriculum, qualifications, language support, safeguarding, communication with parents, activities and the complete cost.

Compare the arrangements actually offered to your child, rather than choosing solely by the school’s language label.
