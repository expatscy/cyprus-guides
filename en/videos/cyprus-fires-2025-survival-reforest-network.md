## Dated evidence about the fire

In a report published on **25 July 2025**, the [ERATOSTHENES Centre of Excellence](https://eratosthenes.org.cy/eratosthenes-new-satellite-data-reveal-the-extent-of-the-limassol-wildfire/) estimated approximately **125 km²** burned in the Limassol district. It used PlanetScope imagery captured on **24 July 2025 at 09:00 UTC** and explicitly described the fire as still active. This was an initial assessment, subject to revision, rather than a final area measurement.

## Reforest Network: goals and published status

The [project website](https://reforest.network/) describes a digital platform intended to connect forest-restoration organisations, volunteers and eco-sponsors in Cyprus. Its stated goals include collaboration and transparency; these descriptions do not establish restoration outcomes.

The website offers a **browser map**. Its demo section describes real satellite imagery and elevation data alongside manually added demonstration data, including dummy wildfires. The project’s [January 2026 version 0.6 update](https://reforest.network/news/update-0-6-progress-features-vision/) reports map navigation and data-layer changes, and lists user registration, organisation editing and native iOS/Android apps among future priorities. Confirm the latest features with the project; the demo is not an official emergency-alert service.

The website identifies **Mellow** as its first sponsor. This corrects the “Mow” name in the interview’s publisher description.

## Assistance and emergency contact

The guest discusses payment delays and criticises the response. Those are his accounts, rather than independently verified findings about every affected household or insurance settlement. The government published [support-measure explanations on 30 July 2025](https://www.gov.cy/ypourgiko-symvoulio/epexigiseis-gia-tis-apofaseis-tou-ypourgikou-symvouliou-gia-stirixi-ton-pligenton-apo-tin-pyrkagia-stin-eparchia-lemesou-30-iouliou-2025/). An announced measure alone does not show whether an individual payment was received.

The [Cyprus Fire Service’s 8 May 2026 announcement](https://www.gov.cy/dikaiosyni-kai-dimosia-taxi/anakoinosi-schetika-me-tis-kliseis-ektaktis-anagkis-pou-apaiteitai-i-antapokrisi-tis-pyrosvestikis-ypiresias/) says that all emergency calls requiring its response, including fires and rescues, should be made **exclusively to 112**.
