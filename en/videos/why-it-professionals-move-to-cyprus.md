## Tax planning alongside the video

The video was uploaded on **16 October 2024**. Check current rules separately before making a relocation or tax decision.

- **Non-dom:** The [Tax Department’s domicile guidance](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/tax-residency-domicility/) describes the conditional SDC exemption on dividends and interest. Deemed domicile applies after Cyprus tax residence in at least **17 of the 20 years immediately preceding the tax year**; individual residence and domicile history matters.
- **IP Box:** The [Ministry of Finance](https://www.gov.cy/mof/documents/forologiki-politiki-kai-metarrythmiseis-2/forologika-kinitra/) describes an **80% deduction** for qualifying IP profit under the **Nexus** calculation and eligible-asset conditions. With the **2026 standard corporate rate of 15%**, full qualification gives the arithmetic **20% × 15% = 3%** of qualifying profit. Check the statutory conditions for your activity.
- **Work and residence:** Use the [residence-permit guide](/en/immigration/residence-permit-cyprus-guide/) and confirm the applicable permission before arranging a move.
