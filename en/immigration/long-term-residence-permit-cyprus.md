> **Short answer:** Cyprus long-term resident status normally requires five years of qualifying legal residence, sufficient stable resources, health insurance, Greek A2 and basic knowledge of Cyprus's political and social reality. The status is permanent subject to statutory loss and withdrawal rules; the residence card lasts five years. It permits employment and self-employment in Cyprus subject to reserved-activity restrictions and offers conditional mobility to participating EU countries.

The route is for third-country nationals legally resident in the government-controlled areas of Cyprus. A valid residence permit is required when applying. See the [residence permits guide](/en/immigration/residence-permit-cyprus-guide/) for other categories, but check whether your actual residence falls within the long-term resident rules.

## What is LTRP?

Cyprus implements the [EU Long-Term Residents Directive 2003/109/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02003L0109-20110520) through [sections 18Z–18KH of the Aliens and Immigration Law](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/0_105/full.html). The Directive's residence arrangements apply in participating EU countries; **Denmark and Ireland are excluded**, as explained by the [European Commission](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/migration-and-asylum/eu-immigration-portal/already-eu_en).

### LTRP vs investor permanent residency

| Aspect | Cyprus LTRP | Regulation 6(2) investor residence |
|--------|-------------|-----------------------------------|
| Main basis | Qualifying residence plus the statutory eligibility conditions | A qualifying investment plus the scheme's other conditions |
| Property option | Property purchase is not an eligibility requirement | First-sale house or apartment of at least €300,000 plus VAT; other qualifying investment categories also exist |
| Cyprus work rights | Employment and self-employment, subject to public-authority and reserved-activity restrictions | Employment restricted, with specified directorship exceptions |
| EU mobility | Application for residence in another participating state, subject to its conditions | No residence entitlement under the Long-Term Residents Directive |

Under the [investor policy](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/documents/etaireies-ependytes-monimi-diamoni/adeies-metanasteysis-ependyton/), the required secured annual income is at least €50,000, increased by €15,000 for a spouse and €10,000 for each minor child. The applicant and spouse must declare no intention to work in Cyprus, except as directors of the company in which they invest under the scheme; where the investment is not in company capital, shareholding and unpaid directorships in Cyprus companies are permitted. These are investor-scheme conditions, separate from LTRP.

## What are the requirements?

### Residence duration and absences

The ordinary rule is **five years of legal, continuous qualifying residence immediately before submission**, in the government-controlled areas. Under section 18H:

- Each absence must be **shorter than six consecutive months**, and total absences must not exceed **ten months** in the relevant five years.
- The Immigration Control Committee may exceptionally treat total absences exceeding ten months as non-interrupting where caused by serious health reasons or duties abroad for a Cyprus-based employer, provided total absences do not exceed 24 months.

### Which residence qualifies?

Permit labels alone do not establish that all time qualifies. Section 18Z excludes students and vocational trainees; persons with protection other than international protection, including temporary protection, or awaiting that type of permission; asylum applicants awaiting a final decision; residence solely for temporary reasons, cross-border services or formally restricted permits; and specified Vienna Convention diplomatic statuses. Periods in the excluded categories listed in section 18H(2)(a) do not count.

For holders of international protection, section 18H counts half the period between the successful protection application and the relevant residence permit, or the whole period if it exceeds 18 months. This differs from temporary protection.

**EU Blue Card mobility exception:** under section 18TZ, a holder who used the statutory long-term mobility route may aggregate five years of qualifying residence in different EU states as a Blue Card holder, highly qualified worker, researcher or international protection beneficiary, with **two immediately preceding years in Cyprus as a Blue Card holder**. Absences from the EU must each be shorter than 12 consecutive months and total no more than 18 months in the relevant aggregation period. Other eligibility conditions still apply.

### Resources, insurance and integration

Section 18Th requires all of the following:

- Stable, regular resources sufficient for the applicant and dependent family without reliance on Cyprus social assistance. Assessment includes employment or other stable lawful income, living costs, appropriate accommodation and, where applicable, an open-ended or at least 18-month employment contract or the viability of intended self-employment.
- Sickness insurance covering the risks normally insured for Cypriot citizens. The acquisition checklist requires an inpatient and outpatient health insurance policy; it does not list GESY registration alone as an alternative.
- **Greek A2** and basic knowledge of Cyprus's contemporary political and social reality. The Committee may grant an exemption in exceptional cases. The checklist asks for the corresponding language and civic knowledge certificates; this is separate from [citizenship language requirements](/en/immigration/greek-language-exam-citizenship/).
- No threat to public order or public security, and no residence obtained through fraud or false representations.

## How does the application process work?

### Required documents

Use **MLT3** and the [official acquisition checklist dated 6 March 2025](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/174/2025/12/New-long-term-eng_06032025.docx), available through the [current forms page](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/en/documents/long-term-residents-lt/). The checklist specifies:

- Passport or other travel document valid for **at least two years**, with certified copies of all relevant arrival/departure pages, and a valid Cyprus residence permit.
- Open-ended or at least **18-month employment contract**, or a self-employment statement describing the intended activity and expected income.
- Original criminal record certificate **issued by Cyprus** and an inpatient/outpatient health insurance policy covering Cyprus.
- Brief CV including email and telephone; translated true copies of academic/vocational qualifications, experience and applicable professional licences.
- Greek A2 and basic political/social knowledge certificates.
- **Twelve months of bank statements**, other-source income certificates and declarations, with an affidavit if necessary.
- Housing title, or where not yet issued the deposited sale deed and proof of payment; alternatively a valid rental agreement with accommodation description and **12 months of rent receipts**, accompanied by telephone, electricity and water bills.
- **Five years of tax declarations and tax clearance**; VAT declarations and clearance where subject to VAT; five years of Social Insurance statements. Section 18I makes Social Insurance evidence applicable where contributions are legally compulsory.
- Duly certified and translated marriage or divorce certificate, as applicable. The MLT3 instructions also ask for **two recent photographs**.

For a Cyprus Blue Card holder who obtained that card through long-term mobility, the checklist instead specifies relevant EU residence documents and EU arrival/departure pages, and **two years** of tax, applicable VAT and Social Insurance records. Read the applicable branch of the checklist rather than using the ordinary five-year document list.

The checklist's older stamp wording must be read with the [Tax Department's notice](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/documents/chartosima/): stamp duty was abolished from 1 January 2026, but documents signed by even one contracting party by 31 December 2025 remain subject to it.

### Where to apply and biometrics

The [acquisition procedure](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/en/documents/acquisition-of-the-status-of-long-term-resident-in-the-republic-2/) requires initial applications with the required original, officially translated and duly certified documents **only at the Migration Department in Nicosia**. Incomplete applications are not accepted. Notify the Department immediately of changes to your details during examination.

The [appointment instructions](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/en/documents/appointments/) list **mageorgiou@md.mip.gov.cy** for arranging or amending Long-Term Resident Status appointments. This category remains centrally handled even for applicants living in another district.

Fingerprints, photograph and signature are required for the card. With a valid travel document and application receipt, biometrics may be captured at submission or later at the Department in Nicosia or the district Police Aliens and Immigration unit. Approval brings a collection notice; rejection brings a letter explaining the reasons.

### Fees and processing

The published **application fee is €30**. This is the submission fee, not an estimate of translation, insurance or other preparation costs.

The Department publishes an **examination time of 6–12 months**. Section 18I requires written notification of the decision within **six months of submission**, extendable by **another six months in exceptional cases involving complex examination**, with notification to the applicant. These are the published estimate and the legal decision rule, respectively.

## What rights and renewal obligations follow approval?

### Permanent status and card renewal

The status is permanent, but the **card is valid for five years** and must be renewed. The [renewal page](https://www.gov.cy/mip-md/en/documents/acquisition-of-the-status-of-long-term-resident-in-the-republic/) says to apply three months before expiry; its online service accepts applications **90–30 days before expiry** with a verified Cy Login account. Section 18IB states renewal application is due no later than three months before expiry. Apply at the earlier statutory point and follow the current service instructions.

Under sections 18IG–18ID, status can be lost or withdrawn for relevant fraud, expulsion or public-order grounds, **12 consecutive months outside the EU**, **six consecutive years outside Cyprus**, or acquisition of long-term resident status in another member state. Expiry of the card alone does not revoke the status. A separate **24-month outside-EU absence rule** applies to holders of the specially marked former-Blue-Card long-term resident permit and their family members granted EU long-term resident status, under section 18TZ(4).

### Cyprus work rights

Section 18IZ provides equal treatment with Cypriot citizens for employment and self-employment. Exceptions cover activities involving public authority and activities reserved by Cyprus or EU law to Cypriot or EU citizens. It is therefore inaccurate to promise unrestricted access to every sector.

### Residence elsewhere in the EU

Articles 14–15 of the Directive allow residence exceeding three months in a **second participating member state**, subject to the relevant application and eligibility conditions. The receiving country may assess resources, insurance, accommodation and employment or self-employment requirements, including labour-market conditions. Apply as soon as possible and no later than three months after entry; the state may accept an application before departure from Cyprus. LTRP does not automatically authorize residence or work throughout the EU.

## How does LTRP relate to citizenship?

LTRP and citizenship have separate tests. Ordinary naturalisation requires **seven qualifying years within the preceding ten before a separate final 12-month period**, plus the applicable Greek B1 or education exemption, civic knowledge, character, resources and other conditions. Qualifying highly skilled employees have a separate reduced-residence route. See the [standard naturalisation guide](/en/immigration/cyprus-citizenship-naturalization-standard/) and [highly skilled route](/en/immigration/cyprus-citizenship-naturalization/).

Changing a residence permit does not itself erase otherwise qualifying residence. Whether earlier employment or LTRP periods count for citizenship depends on the citizenship law's legal residence, physical presence and exclusion rules; LTRP approval alone does not establish citizenship eligibility.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long do you need to live in Cyprus to get LTRP?

The ordinary rule is five years of legal, continuous qualifying residence immediately before applying. Each absence must be shorter than six consecutive months, with no more than ten months in total. Statutory exceptions and separate EU Blue Card mobility rules can change the calculation.

### What is the difference between LTRP and property-based permanent residency?

LTRP requires qualifying residence and other eligibility conditions, provides employment and self-employment rights in Cyprus subject to reserved-activity restrictions, and offers conditional residence in participating EU countries. Regulation 6(2) investor residence has investment and other conditions; it provides no mobility entitlement under the Long-Term Residents Directive and restricts employment, with specified directorship exceptions.

### How long does LTRP processing take in Cyprus?

The Migration Department publishes an examination time of 6–12 months. Section 18I of the Aliens and Immigration Law requires a written decision within six months of submission, extendable by another six months in exceptional complex cases with notification.
