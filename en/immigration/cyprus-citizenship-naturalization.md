This guide covers the differentiated naturalisation qualifications for **highly skilled employees of specified companies** and qualifying family members under section **111B(2)–(3) of Civil Registry Law 141(I)/2002**. Reduced residence qualifications and an accelerated examination are separate parts of the route; neither guarantees citizenship.

> **Official sources:** [Current consolidated law](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_141/full.html), [Ministry of Interior M127 guidance](https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/documents/acquisition-of-cypriot-citizenship-by-naturalization-due-to-years-of-residence-form-m127/), [Greek guidance](https://www.gov.cy/moi/documents/apoktisi-kypriakis-ypikootitas-me-politografisi-logo-eton-diamonis-aitisi-m127/) and [supporting-document checklist](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/21/2025/10/List-of-Supporting-Documents-Application-M127-EN.pdf), checked **3 October 2026**.

## How does this compare to standard naturalisation?

| Requirement | Highly skilled route | Standard residence route |
|---|---|---|
| Earlier qualifying period | 3 years with B1 or the education exemption; 4 with A2 | 7 years |
| Window for the earlier period | 10 years preceding the separate final twelve months | Same window |
| Final period | 12 months legal and continuous residence; up to 90 days' absence does not interrupt it | Same final-period rule |
| Language | A2 or B1 as applicable, or qualifying education exemption | B1 or qualifying education exemption |
| Submission | M127, EUR 500 | M127, EUR 500 |
| Accelerated examination | Eligible applications: additional EUR 5,000; statutory maximum 8 months | Not the ordinary standard-route procedure |
| Certificate after approval | EUR 500 | EUR 500 |

See the [standard naturalisation guide](/en/immigration/cyprus-citizenship-naturalization-standard/) for that route.

## Who can apply?

The Ministry requires the employee to work in an eligible company **both when applying and when the application is examined**. It also requires at least **two years' employment in a qualifying company before submission**. Other cumulative citizenship qualifications still apply.

### Employee Categories

The official guidance describes:

1. Holders of temporary residence permits for employment in **companies of foreign interests**, in the **Director, Specialist or Key Personnel** categories.
2. Individuals, regardless of residence status, receiving a minimum **EUR 2,500 gross monthly salary** and **either** holding a university diploma, degree or equivalent qualification **or** providing certificates of two years' experience in a corresponding position. Tax declarations are given as an evidence example.

The salary alone is insufficient. The company and other eligibility conditions must also be met.

### Eligible Company Types

The Ministry ties eligibility to **Council of Ministers Decision 92.018 of 15 October 2021** and lists:

- Businesses of foreign interests operating independent offices in suitable Cyprus premises, separate from a private residence or another office.
- Cypriot shipping companies.
- Cypriot high-tech or innovation companies in the specified product sectors, including aerospace, computers, IT and communications, pharmaceuticals, biomedical and R&D equipment, electrical machinery, chemicals and non-electrical machinery.
- Cypriot pharmaceutical companies and companies active in biogenetics or biotechnology.
- Companies already registered in the foreign-company register of the Civil Registry and Migration Department, as named in the guidance.
- Accredited international higher and tertiary education institutions. The **Greek Ministry guidance specifies employment of teaching staff** for this category.

For the second employee category, the guidance requires a company-eligibility certificate from the **Business Facilitation Unit or Migration Department**. The supporting-document checklist expressly says this certificate item **does not apply to holders of residence permits with BCS status**. Use the applicable category rather than assuming every applicant needs the same certificate.

## What residence is required?

- With **A2 Greek**: at least **four qualifying years** within the ten years preceding the final twelve-month period.
- With **B1 Greek**, or the specified Greek-medium high-school or academic-degree exemption: at least **three qualifying years** in that earlier window.
- Then **twelve months of legal and continuous residence immediately before application**. Absences totalling no more than **90 days** do not interrupt this final period.
- For the earlier three- or four-year period, the law also counts absences not exceeding **90 days annually** rather than treating them as absence.

Residence means **physical presence in the Republic under the applicable immigration formalities**. Student-permit periods, international-protection applicant periods, and subsidiary- or temporary-protection periods are expressly excluded. The law does not state that all other earlier qualifying residence must start on a Cyprus work-permit date. Keep a detailed arrival and departure record and have your permit history assessed before filing.

The revised qualifications commenced on **19 December 2023**. Earlier qualifying periods may be counted under the statutory conditions. Applications already pending then use the rules applicable when filed; applications submitted after commencement use the law applicable when examined.

## What examinations and other qualifications apply?

The Ministry accepts the specified **Greek Language Centre examinations** conducted through the Ministry of Education's Examination Service or a **University of Cyprus School of Modern Greek** certificate.

The language-certificate exemption is for a recognised **Greek-medium high-school diploma** or an academic degree from a **Greek-medium programme**. Section 111B(2) assigns those exempt qualifications the earlier **three-year** residence period. It is not an exemption for every diploma from any Greek-medium course.

The citizenship knowledge examination requires a certificate scoring **at least 60%** on the basic elements of contemporary Cypriot political and social reality. The law's three-member committee determines the assessment procedure; it is not described as a panel conducting an individual interview.

Applicants must also satisfy **good character**, suitable accommodation, stable and regular resources sufficient for themselves and dependent family members, and the intention-to-reside or public-service requirement. The law's good-character considerations extend beyond a criminal-record certificate. Section 111 gives the Minister discretion; eligibility does not guarantee approval.

See the [Greek examination guide](/en/immigration/greek-language-exam-citizenship/) and [Greek learning guide](/en/life/learning-greek-language-cyprus/) for preparation.

## What documents do I need?

Follow the [M127 checklist](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/21/2025/10/List-of-Supporting-Documents-Application-M127-EN.pdf):

- Original birth certificate.
- Original **Cyprus** clean criminal-record certificate, valid for **six months**.
- Copy of valid residence permit and valid passport or other travel document.
- Marriage certificate or civil-partnership agreement, where applicable.
- Passport arrival and departure stamp copies and a detailed travel record.
- The applicable Greek certificate or qualifying education document and citizenship knowledge-exam certificate.
- Housing evidence, such as title deed, purchase agreement and payment receipts, rental agreement and rent receipts, and utility-payment receipts.
- Financial evidence, such as payroll records, employment contract, social-insurance contributions, income-tax records, bank accounts or other income evidence.
- Company-status certificate where required for the applicable employee category.
- Two recent passport-sized photographs certified by the Community Leader.
- Receipts for the **EUR 500** submission and **EUR 5,000** accelerated-examination fees.

Documents must be duly certified and translated as applicable. For certified translations in Cyprus, use the government's [Sworn Translators service](https://www.gov.cy/en/service/certified-translations-for-official-use/).

The official form has **three guarantor sections**. Each guarantor declares that they acquired Cypriot citizenship otherwise than by naturalisation or registration, are not the applicant's lawyer, agent or relative, and know the applicant personally. The Ministry instructs applicants to sign in a Registrar's presence. The linked form still contains older residence wording; use current law and guidance for the applicable qualifications and ask how to complete those fields.

### Newspaper Publication

The checklist requires copies of notices in **two consecutive publications of a daily newspaper**, notifying submission of the naturalisation application. Use the official form's notice wording and arrange publication with your chosen newspaper.

## How much does it cost?

| Stage | Official fee |
|---|---|
| Submission of M127 | **EUR 500** |
| Accelerated examination of an eligible application | **EUR 5,000** |
| Naturalisation certificate, if approved | **EUR 500** |
| Sum of these three fees | **EUR 6,000** |

This is not a fixed total budget. Confirm other costs and current stamp or certification charges with the District Administration. The Ministry page still lists two EUR 8.54 stamps; the Tax Department confirms repeal of the Stamp Duties Laws from **1 January 2026**, while documents first signed by a party by **31 December 2025** remain subject to the historical rules. This guide does not assume that repeal determines every court or administrative certification charge.

For a pending standard application, ask the District Administration about the correct procedure and fees if your circumstances change. The retrieved primary guidance does not establish a universal withdrawal requirement or a guaranteed **EUR 4,500** upgrade payment.

## How do I submit and follow my application?

Submit **M127 at the local District Administration Office**, with the necessary supporting documents and fee receipts. Confirm appointments and payment arrangements with the office.

Eligible highly skilled employees and their qualifying family members may request **accelerated examination not exceeding eight months**. This statutory examination period is not a guaranteed passport-in-hand date. Follow any requests for further information and the authority's decision instructions.

If approved, the Ministry lists a **EUR 500** certificate fee. Section 111 requires the naturalisation certificate and formal assurance of allegiance for citizenship. Follow the authority's subsequent identity-document instructions; the retrieved guidance does not support fixed certificate-delivery or passport-booking dates for every case.

## How do family applications work?

The family category includes:

- A spouse or civil partner at the time the principal submits their application. Qualifying family members may apply together with or after the principal. Spouses and civil partners must meet the corresponding residence and Greek-language requirements; their qualifying residence periods need not coincide with the principal's.
- A **dependent adult child** whose disability makes them **incapable of employment and legal acts**.

Section 111B(3) also covers a person who was a minor when the parent applied under the highly skilled route and turns eighteen before the parent's naturalisation. That person may apply subject only to the good-character criterion; the application is examined within **three months**, with an extension possible because of the application's particular circumstances.

Ask the District Administration which form and category apply to each child rather than treating every adult child as falling under the disability route.

## Is dual citizenship allowed?

Cyprus law does not impose a universal renunciation requirement, but section 111 permits the Council of Ministers, on the Minister's proposal, to require renunciation in a particular case or category. Check the law of your existing nationality as well.

For military obligations, review the [military service guide](/en/immigration/military-service-cyprus-children/) and ask the competent authority to assess your circumstances before relying on a particular service period.

## What happened to investment citizenship?

The current law no longer provides the former investment naturalisation route for new applications. Section 111A preserves specific provisions for applications submitted up to **30 October 2020** and retains an exceptional honorary naturalisation power for public-interest cases. Obtaining an investment residence permit does not itself confer citizenship under this route.

## What practical tips should I know?

1. Check the company and employee criteria before planning around reduced residence periods.
2. Keep your detailed arrival and departure record.
3. Use the official document checklist and guarantor declarations.
4. Arrange the required consecutive daily-newspaper announcements.
5. Confirm fees and follow-up instructions with the District Administration.
