> **Short answer:** The standard residence route requires at least seven qualifying years in the ten years preceding a separate final twelve-month period, B1 Greek or the specified education exemption, the citizenship knowledge exam, good character, suitable accommodation and sufficient stable financial resources.

This guide covers standard naturalisation under **sections 111 and 111B of Civil Registry Law 141(I)/2002**. The Ministry of Interior states that the revised qualifications took effect on **19 December 2023**. Existing applications pending when that amendment took effect are dealt with under the rules applicable when they were submitted. Applications submitted after the amendment took effect are assessed under the law applicable when they are examined.

> **Official sources:** [Current consolidated law](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_141/full.html), [M127 guidance](https://www.gov.cy/moi/en/documents/acquisition-of-cypriot-citizenship-by-naturalization-due-to-years-of-residence-form-m127/), and [supporting-document checklist](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/21/2025/10/List-of-Supporting-Documents-Application-M127-EN.pdf), checked **3 October 2026**.

## What are the residency requirements?

### Time in Cyprus

- **Twelve months** of legal and continuous residence immediately before application.
- During the **ten years preceding that twelve-month period**, at least **seven years** of qualifying legal residence or service in the Republic's public service, or a combination of the two.
- In the final twelve months, absences totalling **no more than 90 days** do not interrupt the period.

The seven-year period and final twelve months are separate. Keep a detailed record of arrivals and departures; the official checklist requires it.

### Greek Language Requirement

The standard route requires **B1 Greek**. The official guidance accepts the Greek Language Centre examinations conducted through the Ministry of Education's Examination Service, or a certificate from the **University of Cyprus School of Modern Greek**.

The certificate obligation does not apply to holders of a high-school diploma from a recognised secondary institution whose main language of instruction is Greek, or an academic degree from a Greek-medium programme. A diploma from any Greek-medium course is not automatically this exemption.

See the [Greek language exam guide](/en/immigration/greek-language-exam-citizenship/) for preparation information.

### Culture and Politics Exam

The official requirement is a certificate with a score of **at least 60%** in the Examination Service's examination on the basic elements of contemporary Cypriot political and social reality. The law assigns a three-member committee to determine the assessment procedure; it does not describe an individual interview by that panel.

### Other Requirements

- **Good character.** The law sets out relevant considerations, including specified serious convictions, pending criminal proceedings, sanctions, immigration violations and risks to public order or security. This is broader than a clean criminal-record certificate and does not mean every conviction automatically bars an application.
- **Suitable accommodation** and **stable, regular resources** sufficient for the applicant and dependent family members.
- Intention to reside in the Republic or enter or continue its public service.

The law also directs the assessment to consider stable lawful income, long-term unemployment and financial assistance received because of hardship. Meeting the listed qualifications does not guarantee approval: section 111 gives the Minister discretion.

## Which residence periods count?

Section 111B defines residence for naturalisation as **physical presence in the Republic under the applicable immigration formalities**. Holding a permit alone does not establish every day of qualifying presence.

The law expressly excludes periods spent as:

- An applicant for international protection.
- A holder of subsidiary protection.
- A holder of temporary protection.
- A student-permit holder.

Ask the District Administration to assess your immigration history rather than assuming every permit or every absence counts. The law allows qualifying periods and other qualifications to have been acquired before or after the 2023 amendment took effect.

## What documents are required?

Use the [current M127 checklist](https://www.gov.cy/media/sites/21/2025/10/List-of-Supporting-Documents-Application-M127-EN.pdf). Supporting documents must be duly certified and translated as applicable. For certified translations in Cyprus, the [government service](https://www.gov.cy/en/service/certified-translations-for-official-use/) directs applicants to the Sworn Translators Register.

### Application Form

Use **M127** and sign in the presence of a Registrar as instructed by the Ministry. The linked form has three guarantor sections. Each guarantor declares that they acquired Cypriot citizenship otherwise than by naturalisation or registration, are not the applicant's lawyer, agent or relative, and know the applicant personally.

The linked form contains older residence wording. Use the current law and Ministry guidance for the applicable residence qualifications and ask the District Administration how to complete those fields.

### Personal Documents

The checklist includes:

- Original birth certificate.
- Original **Cyprus** clean criminal-record certificate, valid for **six months**.
- Copy of valid residence permit.
- Marriage certificate or civil-partnership agreement, where applicable.
- Copy of valid passport or other travel document.
- Copies of passport pages with arrival and departure stamps.
- Detailed arrival and departure record.
- Two recent passport-sized photographs certified by the Community Leader.
- Copy of the **EUR 500** application-fee receipt.

### Exams and Financial Evidence

- The applicable language certificate or qualifying education document.
- The citizenship knowledge-exam certificate showing **60% or above**.
- Housing evidence, such as title deed, purchase agreement and payment receipts, rental agreement and rent receipts, and utility-payment receipts.
- Financial evidence, such as payroll records, employment contract, social-insurance contributions, income-tax records, bank accounts or other income evidence.

These are evidence examples; the checklist does not prescribe fixed one-year or five-year periods for every financial record.

### Newspaper Publication

Provide copies of announcements in **two consecutive publications of a daily newspaper**, notifying submission of the naturalisation application. Use the notice wording in the official form and confirm the publication arrangements with your chosen newspaper.

## How much does it cost?

The Ministry lists:

| Stage | Fee |
|---|---|
| Submission of M127 | **EUR 500** |
| Naturalisation certificate after approval | **EUR 500** |

These fees do not establish a total budget. Translation, certification, examinations, publication and later passport or ID applications may have separate costs. Confirm current stamp and certification charges with the District Administration: the Ministry page still lists two EUR 8.54 stamps, while the Tax Department states that the Stamp Duties Laws were repealed from **1 January 2026**. Do not treat the old stamp amount as a confirmed current requirement.

## Where do I apply?

Submit **M127 at the local District Administration Office**, with the required supporting documents and fees. Ask that office about appointment and payment arrangements.

## How long does processing take?

The official M127 guidance does not give a standard-route completion estimate. Plan around the progress of your own application rather than a promised date.

A separate category exists for qualifying highly skilled employees of specified companies and their qualifying family members. It uses **four qualifying years with A2 Greek or three with B1**, before the separate final twelve months, subject to the other eligibility conditions. The Ministry says applicants in that employment category must have worked for a qualifying company for **at least two years before applying**. An accelerated examination of eligible applications can be requested for **EUR 5,000**, with the statutory period not exceeding **eight months**. See the [highly skilled naturalisation guide](/en/immigration/cyprus-citizenship-naturalization/) for the full criteria.

## How do family applications work?

Do not assume a standard-route application automatically covers a spouse or gives parents a reduced residence period. Ask the District Administration which citizenship category applies to each family member. Citizenship through marriage and registration based on Cypriot descent use separate criteria.

## What happens after approval?

The Ministry lists a further **EUR 500** fee for issuing the naturalisation certificate. Section 111 provides that citizenship follows the certificate and the formal assurance of allegiance. Follow the District Administration's instructions for the remaining steps and subsequent identity-document applications.

For military obligations, review the [military service guide](/en/immigration/military-service-cyprus-children/) and seek the competent authority's assessment before relying on a particular service period.

## Is dual citizenship allowed?

The UK permits dual nationality and says British citizens can acquire another nationality while retaining British citizenship. Cyprus law does not impose a universal renunciation requirement, but **section 111** allows the Council of Ministers, on the Minister's proposal, to make renunciation a condition in a particular case or category. Check your existing nationality's rules as well.

## What was the investment-based citizenship route?

The current law no longer provides the former investment naturalisation route for new applications. Section 111A preserves specific provisions for applications submitted up to **30 October 2020** and retains a separate, exceptional honorary naturalisation power for public-interest cases. Purchasing property or obtaining an investment residence permit does not itself confer citizenship under the standard route.

## What practical tips should I know?

1. Plan for the applicable Greek-language and citizenship knowledge examinations.
2. Keep the detailed arrival and departure record required by the checklist.
3. Prepare income and housing evidence suited to your circumstances.
4. Arrange the two consecutive daily-newspaper announcements using the official notice format.
5. Read the guarantor declarations before asking anyone to sign.
6. Confirm current fees, certification instructions and appointment arrangements with your District Administration.
